# User stories
- US-1: As an employee, I create an expense report with line items.
- US-2: As an employee, I upload a receipt photo and see the total auto-filled.
- US-3: As an employee, I submit a report and see whether it violates policy.
- US-4: As a manager, I see a queue of reports awaiting my approval.
- US-5: As a manager, I approve or reject a report with a comment.
- US-6: As an employee, I get an email when my report is approved or rejected.
- US-7: As an admin, I set spending limits per category.
- US-8: As payroll, I receive a nightly file of approved reports.
- US-9: As a manager's manager, I get reports escalated to me after 3 business days.
