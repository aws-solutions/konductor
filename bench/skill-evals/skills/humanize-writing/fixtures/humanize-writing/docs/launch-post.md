# Introducing Relay 2.4 — A New Era of Seamless Deployments

In today's rapidly evolving landscape, deployment tooling stands as a pivotal cornerstone of modern engineering. Relay 2.4 is not just an update — it's a testament to our unwavering commitment to developer experience. We're thrilled to delve into what makes this release so transformative.

At its core, Relay 2.4 boasts a robust, reimagined build cache that makes incremental deploys 37% faster on our internal benchmark of 1,800 services. Additionally, the cache now persists across runners, ensuring teams never rebuild what hasn't changed, fostering a culture of speed, efficiency, and innovation.

Furthermore, Relay now supports 12 regions out of the box — from Sydney to São Paulo — empowering global teams to deploy closer to their users. This seamless expansion underscores the intricate interplay between performance and reach.

Security has also been enhanced. Relay 2.4 introduces signed deployment manifests, so every artifact can be traced to the commit that produced it. It’s a game-changer for compliance teams navigating the complexities of SOC 2 audits.

The future looks bright. We can’t wait to see what you build with Relay 2.4, and we're excited to continue this incredible journey together! Let us know what you think — we hope this helps you unlock your team's full potential.
