# Plain Writing Habits

## Short Summaries

Lead with the result. Give one or two sentences first and add detail only if the reader asks for it.

## Plain Names

Pick a name that says what the thing holds or does. Avoid single letters outside a short loop, and avoid abbreviations a new teammate would have to look up.

## Error Messages

Say what failed, include the value involved, and say what the reader can do next.
