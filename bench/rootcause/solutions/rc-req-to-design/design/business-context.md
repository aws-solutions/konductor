# Business context for the design

## Product

A tool library for neighbors: members borrow tools from a shared stock instead of buying them.

## Problem

The library has 1,200 tools and 4,000 members, and staff track loans on paper. Members wait in line, tools get lost and nobody can tell what is available.

## Users and scale

- Members search from a phone or computer and check out at a kiosk.
- Staff and volunteers run the desk and manage the catalog.
- Peak: Saturday 9:00 to 12:00, up to 150 checkouts an hour (about 2.5 a minute).

## Success measures

- Checkout takes under 2 minutes at the kiosk.
- Staff time on paperwork drops by half in six months.

## Out of scope for the first release

Online payments and online collection of fees. Fees are paid at the desk.
