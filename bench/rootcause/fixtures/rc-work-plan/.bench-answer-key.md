# rc-work-plan answer key

Judge-only. Runners must not copy this file into the agent working directory.

Input: `docs/plan.md` and its background `docs/context.md`. Output expected: `review/plan-review.md`, a review of the plan. No change to the inputs. There is no test command.

## Seeded problems

The four columns follow the usual plan-review criteria: clarity, verifiability, context completeness, big picture.

| ID | Criterion | Location | Problem |
|----|-----------|----------|---------|
| PR1 | clarity | task 3 | "Make the exporter better" names no deliverable and no way to tell it is better; it has no dependency either, so it is not clear what it improves. |
| PR2 | verifiability | tasks 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 | "Done when" is empty (2, 7), or cannot be checked: "works", "deployed", "no failures" without a threshold. |
| PR3 | context | tasks 4, 6 | "The standard retry settings" and "the Q3 volumes" are not defined in the plan. The background says retry settings are per service (3 retries, 2 seconds) and never mentions Q3 volumes. |
| PR4 | big picture | tasks 8, 9, 10 | The nightly job is deleted (9) before the month-long reconciliation (8) is done, and there is no rollback, no parallel run and no cutover step. A bad export cannot be undone because the CSV job is gone. |
| PR5 | clarity | task 6 | The load test depends only on task 2 but needs the staging deployment (5). |
| PR6 | big picture | whole plan | The target is 3 weeks (15 working days) but the tasks add up to 36 days, the longest chain is longer than 15 days (1, 7, 8 alone are 17), and a full month of reconciliation cannot finish inside 3 weeks. |
| PR7 | context | tasks 7, 8; Dana | Tasks 7 and 8 have no owner. Dana owns 2, 3 and 6, 12 days, with 2 and 3 free to start together. Fran is away in week 2 and the plan says "helps where needed". |
| PR8 | big picture | task 6 | Month-end volume (about four times normal, the March incident) is not covered; the load test uses other volumes and no check against the queue limit of 500 messages a second. |
| PR9 | big picture | goal, notes | The goal says to move the reports service to the queue, but no task does it and the data team (its owner) is not involved. |
| PR10 | context | task 10, header | Production deploy is planned for a Wednesday; the background says deploys happen on Tuesday and Thursday only. |

## Good review behavior

- Findings point at tasks by number and have a severity. PR4 and PR6 are critical or important; PR1 and PR5 are important or minor.
- A rating out of 5 and a verdict that the plan is not ready (needs fixes, blocked).
- Each finding has a fix.

## False claims to watch

- Says the plan is ready or approves it without conditions.
- Cites a task number that does not exist, or a constraint the background does not state.
- Rewrites the plan files.

## Entry agent and skills the task should reach

- k-tpm: plan-review (primary), risk-management, task-decomposition or program-planning for the fixes.
