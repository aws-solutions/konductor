# rc-product-handoff answer key

Judge-only. Runners must not copy this file into the agent working directory.

Input: `product/requirements.md` (with marketing language) and `product/user-stories.md`. Output expected: design input files under `design/` (business context, requirements, stories boiled down) and a first system design at `design/system-design.md`. No change to the product documents. There is no test command.

## Seeded points

| ID | What a good handoff and design do |
|----|-----------------------------------|
| RQ1 | Keeps the numbers: 4,000 members, 1,200 tools, the Saturday peak of 150 checkouts an hour between 9:00 and 12:00, a 2-minute checkout, 7-day loans. The design sizes against the peak (about 2.5 a minute is small, so a simple design is right) and says so. |
| RQ2 | Finds the offline requirement that sits in the acceptance criteria of US-4, not in the requirements table: the kiosk works 8 hours without a network and syncs later. The design has local storage on the kiosk, a queue of loans and a conflict rule for the sync. |
| RQ3 | Flags the conflict between REQ-6 (reserve up to 30 days ahead) and US-3 (next day only) as an open question, or states which one it assumed and why. It does not silently pick one. |
| RQ4 | Carries the privacy rules: personal data deleted within 30 days of a request, photo ID never stored, loan history kept without the name (US-7). The design separates identity data from loan records so the deletion is possible. |
| RQ5 | Names the two integrations: the nightly CSV from the city membership list (the library does not own identity) and the city's SMS provider for reminders. |
| RQ6 | Treats online payments as out of scope (REQ-12): no payment service in the design. Fees are recorded at the desk (US-8). |
| RQ7 | Carries the accessibility rule for the kiosk: WCAG 2.1 AA and 44 pixel touch targets. |
| RQ8 | Lists the late fee rate as an open question for the board, with the design not depending on a number. |
| RQ9 | The design inputs drop the marketing language (revolutionary, game-changing, best-in-class, the testimonial). |
| RQ10 | The system design traces requirements to components: most REQ ids (at least 10 of 12) appear next to the component or decision that meets them. |

## False claims to watch

- A payment gateway or online fee collection in the design.
- Says the kiosk needs a constant connection, or leaves offline out.
- Invents a late fee amount.
- Quotes the testimonial or the slogans as requirements.

## Entry agent and skills the task should reach

- k-architect: requirements-extraction (primary, the handoff), then system-design-patterns for the design; non-functional-requirements, kiro-design-generation or adr-generator may follow.
