# Community Tool Library: Requirements

## The vision

We are building the revolutionary, game-changing way for neighbors to share tools. "I borrowed a ladder in two minutes and never looked back," says a delighted early member. Our best-in-class experience turns every garage into a shared resource.

## The problem

Neighbors buy tools they use twice a year. The library has 1,200 tools and 4,000 members, and staff track loans on paper. Members wait in line, tools get lost and nobody can tell what is available.

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| REQ-1 | Members can see which tools are available, by name and category, from a phone or a computer. |
| REQ-2 | A member can check out a tool at the kiosk by scanning a member card and the tool label. |
| REQ-3 | Staff can add, retire and repair-flag tools. |
| REQ-4 | A tool is lent for 7 days and can be renewed once. |
| REQ-5 | A late tool earns a late fee per day. The fee rate is to be decided by the board. |
| REQ-6 | Members can reserve a tool up to 30 days ahead. |
| REQ-7 | Members get a text message reminder 1 day before a loan is due, through the city's SMS provider. |
| REQ-8 | Membership comes from the city membership list, delivered as a CSV file every night. The library does not own member identity. |
| REQ-9 | A member's personal data is deleted within 30 days of a request. Photo ID is never stored. |
| REQ-10 | The kiosk meets WCAG 2.1 AA and has touch targets of at least 44 pixels. |
| REQ-11 | Saturday morning is the peak: up to 150 checkouts an hour between 9:00 and 12:00. |
| REQ-12 | Online payments and online collection of fees are out of scope for the first release. Fees are paid at the desk. |

## Success

Checkout takes under 2 minutes at the kiosk. Staff time on paperwork drops by half in six months.
