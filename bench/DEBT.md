## Debt policy

1. Fix it in the same change. Open a row only for a finding that change cannot fix: it needs a paid run, a host this one is not, an owner's decision, or work much larger than the change. The next action says which.
2. Every row that is not closed has an owner, a next action and a due date. At the weekly review, each past-due row gets a new date with a reason, or is closed.
3. A new task, or a change to a task's checks, key or a scorer, ships with its free gate record (the batch dry run: reference, stub, lazy, alternative answer) and its recheck record (`handEdited` and a non-Claude recheck, or why none is due). It also passes `scenarios lint`. Then a bug class a review found once is caught by a check, and nobody has to find it again.
4. When reviews find the same kind of bug twice, the fix adds a check for the whole class: a lint rule, a shared test helper or a fixture check. Known cases go in that check's baseline with their row, and the baseline only shrinks (`bench/scenarios/lint-baseline.json` is the first).
5. A row with the same cause as another is merged into it with a link, and closed. A row is never deleted. A row is closed with the proof that its closure check passed.
