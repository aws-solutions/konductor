Three people at 10 points each is 30 points. Chi is on leave for 3 working days and one point is one working day, so capacity is 30 - 3 = **27 points**. Already planned: 14 points (T-201 to T-204).

## Load of sprint 15

- What the board will show once the rollover has run: 14 + 3 + 5 + 0 = **22 of 27 points**, 81 percent. The new T-103 has no points and no subtasks.
- The real load: 14 + 3 + 5 + 5 = **27 of 27 points**, 100 percent. The sprint fits exactly, with no headroom. Any new work or an overrun puts it over capacity.

## What the move loses

The Asana integration cannot move a task to another section. The rollover therefore recreates each task in the sprint 15 section and closes the original. A recreated task does not keep its subtasks, so T-103a and T-103b are lost, and so are the 5 points on them. Comments, attachments, followers, tags and the task history are lost too, and only the points on the task itself are copied.

## What to do

1. Warn the team about the data loss and get a yes before running the rollover.
2. After it runs, recreate T-103a and T-103b under the new T-103 with their points (2 and 3) and the due date 2026-03-27, or put the 5 points on the new T-103, so the board shows the real load of 27.
3. Copy any comments or attachments that matter from the original tasks, which stay closed with a link to the new task.
4. Treat sprint 15 as full. Anything new needs something else to leave.
