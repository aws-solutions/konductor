## Planted fact

The Asana integration cannot move a task to another section, so a rollover recreates each task in the new sprint's section. The recreated task loses its subtasks (and its comments, attachments, followers, tags and history). Only the story points of the task itself are copied. This is in `skills/sprint-planning/references/asana.md` (Sprint Rollover) of the folded skill, and in the body of the old standalone Asana skill on main. It is not in the folded `SKILL.md`.

Consequence for this input: T-103 has no points of its own, its 5 points sit on two subtasks, and the recreated T-103 arrives with no subtasks and no points.
