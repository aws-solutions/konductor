# Vision

Members of a small public library often find that a popular title is checked out. Today they ask a librarian to write their name on a paper list. Staff lose track of the list, members wait without knowing where they stand, and returned books sit on the shelf while the next person is never called.

We want members to place a hold online, wait in a fair queue, and be told by email when a copy is ready. Staff need a simple view of each queue.

Scope for the first release: holds on titles with no available copy. Fines, renewals and inter-library loans are out of scope.
