# Answer key: sd-feedback (task sdlc-problem-framing)

The request asks for PDF export. The data says the real problem is that users cannot find the export that exists.

## Seeded facts (ids used by the checks and rubric)

- F1: `data/tickets.csv` has 66 tickets. 41 (62%) are `cant-find-export`, 9 (14%) `pdf-request`, 10 (15%)
  `slow-report`, 6 `other`.
- F2: CSV export exists (release 4.2) and sits under Settings > Advanced > Data tools, not on the report page.
- F3: `data/usage.csv`: about 1,200 weekly active users and 34 to 38 export clicks a week (about 3%).
  `pdf_print_clicks` is 0 every week because the print button was hidden in release 4.6.
- F4: PDF export costs 6 to 8 weeks for two engineers plus a rendering service; moving the CSV button costs 2 days.
- F5: Slow reports (10 tickets) are a separate problem the request does not mention.

## Intended framing

Problem: users cannot find how to get report data out (41 of 66 tickets), not a missing PDF format (9 of 66).
Options: do nothing; put the existing CSV export and the print button on the report page; build PDF export; a
mixed path (move the button now, then measure PDF demand). Recommendation: move the button and restore print
first, measure, and revisit PDF only if PDF tickets stay. Success measure: `cant-find-export` tickets per month
from 41 to under 10, and export clicks from about 3% of weekly users up, read from the ticket export and the usage
file. Stop signal: if `pdf-request` tickets do not fall or PDF tickets rise above a number, revisit PDF.

## Traps

- Planning PDF export as asked, with no look at the data.
- Counting `pdf-request` as the whole demand, or adding the 41 to it.
- Treating the hidden print button as a reason that nobody wants printing (0 clicks means it is hidden).
