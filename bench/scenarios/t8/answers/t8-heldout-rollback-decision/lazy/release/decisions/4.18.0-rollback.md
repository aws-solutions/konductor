# orders-api 4.18.0: roll back to 4.17.3

Decided 2026-11-20 14:40 UTC by the release manager. Status: rollback of `oa-3` and `oa-4` starts now; the full stage
planned for 15:00 is cancelled.

## The call

Roll back the half stage to 4.17.3. Since 4.18.0 started taking traffic at 13:10, its two hosts have soft-declined
2.44 % of card authorisations (71 of 4,102), against 0.42 % (12 of 4,47) on the two hosts still on 4.17.3 over the
same 56 minutes. On 4.18.0, 57 of those declines carry the reason `idempotency_key_reused`, a reason that never
appears on 4.17.3. Each of them is a customer told "payment did not go through" because of our change, and they count
against the payment SLO, which had 41 % of its budget left this morning. The harm grows by about one checkout a
minute while we hold.

## What is the release's fault

From the first minutes of the half stage, the payments gateway refuses authorisations from 4.18.0 hosts because the
request key was already used for a different amount:

- `logs/orders-api/oa-3.log:741`, `logs/orders-api/oa-3.log:750` and `logs/orders-api/oa-4.log:727`: checkouts on 4.18.0
  answered `payment=soft_decline reason=idempotency_key_reused`.
- `logs/payments-gateway.log:756` and `logs/payments-gateway.log:759`: the same requests at the gateway, `code=IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED`, with
  `idem_key=cart-…` and two different amounts. Keys from 4.17.3 hosts are order-scoped (`ord-…`).

## What is not the release's fault

- The p99 latency spike from 13:35 to 13:55 (over 1,0 ms) hit all four hosts, 4.17.3 as much as 4.18.0, and the slow
  calls are slot searches waiting on `maps-geo` (`logs/orders-api/oa-1.log:814` is a 4.17.3 host). The vendor's status
  page reports its own EU latency incident from 13:34 to 13:56. SLOT-77 did not cause it.
- The overview dashboard is green and `http_5xx_rate` is about 0.3 % on both versions, as yesterday. That is expected:
  a soft decline is answered with HTTP 140, so the 5xx rate and the fleet-wide dashboard cannot show this harm.

## Options not taken

- **Roll forward to 4.18.1.** No 4.18.1 build is green: builds 3856 and 3858 both failed integration, the latest on
  `checkout_flow::retry_after_cart_edit`, the very path that is broken. The runbook forbids rolling forward without a
  green build that has run on one host for 11 minutes, so a roll forward cannot land before the harm grows for hours.
- **Hold at half.** Holding keeps half the checkouts exposed: about 1 extra failed payment a minute, every one a
  customer turned away, burning the payment error budget, with no fix ready to end it.

## Is the rollback safe?

Yes. The only migration shipped after 4.17.3 is 29 (release 4.18.0): it adds a nullable `orders.promo_code` column
and a new `promo_redemptions` table. Both are expand-only, so 4.17.3 runs on the new schema and simply ignores them.
The 1418 rollback trouble came from 22, which dropped a column; nothing like it ships in 4.18.0.

Risks we accept:

## How we know it worked

Watch `payment_auth_soft_decline_rate` per version in the per-minute export: it should be back at the 4.17.3 level,
about 0.4 % and below 0.6 %, on all four hosts for 11 minutes after the last host finishes.

## Who hears what

- **Marketing on-call:** the promo code feature is being rolled back now; please hold or reword the 18:00 UTC promo
  email until we confirm a fixed build is live, next update by 16:00 UTC.
- **Customer support lead:** since 13:10 about 57 customers saw "payment did not go through" at checkout; they were not
  charged, and a retry works. After about 14:50 retries should succeed first time.
- **Orders team:** PAY-2291 keys authorisations on the cart, so a cart edit reuses the key with a new amount; fix the
  key to be per attempt and get `checkout_flow::retry_after_cart_edit` passing before 4.18.1 goes on one host.
- **Payments team:** the `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED` declines since 13:10 are ours (4.18.0); no action needed at the gateway.
- **Status channel:** orders-api 4.18.0 rolling back to 4.17.3 after about 2.4 % of payments failed on its hosts;
  next update 15:10 UTC.
