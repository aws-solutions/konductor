# Who to tell during a release

| Audience | Reach them | What they need from us |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing on-call | #marketing-oncall | anything that changes what a campaign can promise, before it is sent |
| Customer support lead | #support-leads | what customers see, what to tell them, whether anyone was charged |
| Orders team | #orders-dev | what to fix, what must pass before the next build goes out |
| Payments team | #payments | anything we see from the gateway |
| Status channel | #status | one line: what changed, impact, next update time |
