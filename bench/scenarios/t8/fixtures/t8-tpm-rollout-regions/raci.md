# Release RACI

The role names below are the decision roles for the Aurora Wallet release.

| Work | Accountable | Responsible | Consulted |
|---|---|---|---|
| regional rollout | Release Manager | Release Engineering | Regional Operations |
| customer support cover | Support Lead | Customer Support | Regional Operations |
| residency approval | Privacy Lead | Privacy Engineering | Risk and Compliance |
| partner certification | Partner Lead | Partner Integrations | Risk and Compliance |
| schema change | Data Lead | Data Platform | Edge API |
| final launch acceptance | Product Director | Release Manager | all delivery leads |
