# ROI Analysis - Search Improvements
*Calculated: November 14, 2023*

## Current Costs (Monthly)
- Support tickets: 347 @ 12 min each = 69.4 hours
- Support cost: 69.4 hrs × $45/hr = $3,123
- Lost sales (estimated): $18,500
- **Total monthly impact: $21,623**

## Projected Improvements

### Option 1: Part Number Normalization Only
- **Support reduction**: 50% of part number tickets = 118 tickets
- **Time saved**: 118 × 12 min = 23.6 hours
- **Cost savings**: 23.6 × $45 = $1,062/month
- **Sales recovery**: 30% of lost sales = $5,550/month
- **Total monthly benefit**: $6,612

**Implementation cost**: $4,800 (one-time)
**Monthly ROI**: $6,612 / ($4,800/12 + $0) = 1,653% in first year

### Option 2: Full Search Engine (Algolia)
- **Support reduction**: 70% of all search tickets = 243 tickets
- **Time saved**: 243 × 12 min = 48.6 hours
- **Cost savings**: 48.6 × $45 = $2,187/month
- **Sales recovery**: 50% of lost sales = $9,250/month
- **Total monthly benefit**: $11,437

**Implementation cost**: $15,000 (one-time) + $650/month
**Monthly ROI**: $11,437 / ($15,000/12 + $650) = 610% in first year

### Option 3: PostgreSQL Enhancement Package
- **Support reduction**: 60% of part number tickets = 142 tickets
- **Time saved**: 142 × 12 min = 28.4 hours
- **Cost savings**: 28.4 × $45 = $1,278/month
- **Sales recovery**: 35% of lost sales = $6,475/month
- **Total monthly benefit**: $7,753

**Implementation cost**: $9,600 (one-time) + $186/month (upgrade)
**Monthly ROI**: $7,753 / ($9,600/12 + $186) = 752% in first year

## Payback Period
1. **Normalization only**: 0.7 months
2. **Algolia**: 1.5 months
3. **PostgreSQL enhancement**: 1.3 months

## Risk-Adjusted ROI
Considering implementation risks and uncertainty:
1. **Normalization**: Low risk, high certainty of benefit
2. **Algolia**: Medium risk (vendor lock-in), medium certainty
3. **PostgreSQL enhancement**: Medium risk (complexity), high certainty

## Non-Financial Benefits
1. **Customer satisfaction**: Expected improvement from 2.8 to 4.0/5
2. **Competitive position**: Match basic competitor features
3. **Team morale**: Reduce support team frustration
4. **Strategic flexibility**: Keep options open for future enhancements

## Conclusion
All options show positive ROI with payback <2 months. Part number normalization has best risk-adjusted return and can be implemented quickly while keeping strategic options open.
