# User Interviews - Search Pain Points
*Conducted: November 7-9, 2023 | Participants: 12*

## Participant Profile
- **Professional mechanics**: 5
- **DIY enthusiasts**: 4  
- **Auto shop owners**: 2
- **Parts resellers**: 1

## Key Findings

### Part Number Search Issues
- **All mechanics** reported problems with part number searches
- **Quote**: "I have the exact part number from the manufacturer, but your site can't find it unless I remove the dashes. Every other site handles this."
- **Workaround**: "I keep a notepad of which parts need which format. AP-1234-567 sometimes works as AP1234567, sometimes as 1234567."

### Text Search Problems
- **7 participants** said text search returns irrelevant results
- **Quote**: "Searching for 'brake pads' shows me brake fluid, brake lines, everything but the pads first."
- **Expectation**: "When I search for a specific car part, show me that part first, not accessories."

### Mobile Experience
- **All mobile users** reported slower search
- **Quote**: "On my phone, search takes forever. By the time results load, I've already gone to a competitor."
- **Observation**: Mobile users more likely to abandon search after 2 attempts

### Feature Requests
1. **Autocomplete**: "Show me parts as I type"
2. **Filtering**: "Let me filter by price, brand, availability"
3. **History**: "Remember what I searched for"
4. **Suggestions**: "If part number fails, show similar parts"

## Impact on Behavior

### Professional Users
- **Mechanics**: Spend 15-20 minutes searching vs 5 on competitors
- **Shop owners**: Order from competitors for time-sensitive jobs
- **Resellers**: Use our site only as last resort

### DIY Enthusiasts
- **Frustration level**: High - many give up after failed searches
- **Conversion**: Estimated 40% abandon purchase due to search issues
- **Loyalty**: Low - will try competitors first next time

## Quotes That Stood Out
> "It's 2023. How can an auto parts site not handle dashes in part numbers?"

> "I love your prices, but I can't buy what I can't find."

> "The search feels broken. Like it's working against me, not helping me."

> "I've trained my team to call support instead of using search. That's sad."

## Recommendations from Users
1. **Fix part numbers first** - this is the biggest pain point
2. **Make search faster** - especially on mobile
3. **Add basic features** - autocomplete, filtering
4. **Improve error messages** - tell users why search failed

## Business Impact Estimate
- **Lost professional business**: $45,000/month (based on interviews)
- **DIY conversion improvement**: 25-40% possible with better search
- **Support cost reduction**: 50-70% possible
