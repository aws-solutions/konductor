# Customer Support Complaints - Search Related
*Period: October 1 - November 15, 2023*

## Total Search-Related Tickets: 347

### By Category

**Part Number Issues (68% - 236 tickets)**
- "Part number not found" - 142 tickets
- "System doesn't recognize my part number" - 54 tickets
- "Dashes/spaces in part number don't work" - 28 tickets
- "Leading zeros missing from part number" - 12 tickets

**Search Relevance Issues (22% - 76 tickets)**
- "Wrong results shown" - 31 tickets
- "Too many irrelevant results" - 25 tickets
- "Can't find what I'm looking for" - 20 tickets

**Performance Issues (7% - 24 tickets)**
- "Search is slow" - 18 tickets
- "Timeout errors" - 6 tickets

**Other Issues (3% - 11 tickets)**
- Mobile app search problems - 7 tickets
- Autocomplete not working - 4 tickets

### Key Quotes from Customers

> "I tried AP-1234-567, AP1234567, and AP 1234 567 - none of them work even though I know we stock this part."

> "The search keeps telling me 'no results found' when I search by part number, but if I describe it in words I can sometimes find it."

> "Why can't the system handle basic variations like dashes or spaces? Every other auto parts site does this."

> "I have to call support every time I need to look up a part by number. This is costing us hours every week."

### Impact Analysis

**Support Time Spent:**
- Average resolution time: 12 minutes
- Total support hours: 69.4 hours
- Estimated cost (@ $45/hr): $3,123

**Business Impact:**
- Estimated lost sales: $18,500 (based on ticket follow-ups)
- Customer satisfaction score: 2.8/5 (search category)
- 15 customers switched to competitors citing search issues

### Trends
1. Part number issues increasing month-over-month (+23% from Q3)
2. Mobile users report more problems (72% of mobile searches result in support contact)
3. B2B customers most affected (they primarily search by part number)

### Recommended Actions (Support Team)
1. Implement part number normalization
2. Add search suggestions for common part number formats
3. Create better error messages with formatting tips
4. Consider a dedicated part number search field
