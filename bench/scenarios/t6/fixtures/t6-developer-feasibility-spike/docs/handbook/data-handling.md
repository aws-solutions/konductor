# Data handling (excerpt)

- Customer files never go into a repository, a ticket attachment or a chat. Ask the customer for a
  cut-down copy and delete it when the ticket closes.
- Test data in repos is generated. Generators take their seeds from constants.
- Counts taken from support tooling may be quoted internally with the customer alias, not the name.
- When a number comes from a sample of tickets or files, say how big the sample was.
