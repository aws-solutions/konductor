# Experiment review policy excerpt

A page test may be released only after the assignment distribution is reconciled with the intended split, material campaigns are recorded, and the primary measure is checked against the agreed observation window. A purchase-only read is provisional when refunds normally arrive inside that window.

If a campaign reaches one experience or one source disproportionately, the result must be reported as directional and the team should repeat the comparison without that campaign. The release owner records the decision and the baseline used for monitoring.
