# Pricing page experiment brief

Owner: Mira Sen, Product
Experiment ID: PRC-25-04
Planned dates: 8 April through 21 April 2025

## Why we ran it

The pricing page team wanted to test whether a simpler annual-plan presentation would help more visitors complete a paid checkout. Control kept the existing annual-plan card. Variant B used a shorter headline, showed the monthly equivalent beside the annual price, and put the annual plan first on small screens. The price and checkout flow were intended to stay the same.

## Audience and assignment

The audience was signed-in learners in English-language markets who had not purchased an annual plan in the previous 180 days. Assignment was intended to be an even, persistent split at the learner level. Staff, free-trial renewals, existing subscribers, and learners with a failed payment in the previous seven days were excluded.

## Measurement

The primary metric is paid annual-plan conversion: a learner assigned during the run who records a successful annual-plan payment within seven days. The first purchase event is used for the initial read. The team also records gross revenue, checkout starts, and support contacts. A later refund file should be joined by order ID before a launch decision.

The review window is fourteen calendar days. The brief says to look at the refund-adjusted result before making a permanent page change. No external campaign was planned for the test window when this brief was approved.

## Guardrails

Pause if payment failures, support contacts about price confusion, or seven-day refunds rise by more than the previous-quarter weekly average. Keep the assignment key and page-copy version in the experiment log.
