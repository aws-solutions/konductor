# Competitive Analysis - Calendar SaaS Market

**Prepared by:** Marketing Team  
**Last updated:** May 30, 2025

## Key Competitors

### CalendarPro
- Has CSV export with basic metadata
- **Missing:** Customer ID mapping in exports
- Sales notes: Basic text fields only
- Support tickets: Not integrated

### TeamScheduler  
- Strong export capabilities
- **Has:** Customer ID in CSV exports (their #1 requested feature last year)
- Sales notes: Rich text with attachments
- Support tickets: Basic integration

### SyncCalendar
- Weak on exports
- Sales notes: Advanced with AI summaries
- Support tickets: Full integration (their differentiator)
- **Note:** Customers switching to them for support features

## Market Gaps
1. **Export with customer context** - Only TeamScheduler has this
2. **Sales notes with workflow** - All have basic, none have advanced
3. **Support ticket integration** - SyncCalendar leads here

## Customer Switching Data
- 15% of churn mentions "missing export features"
- 22% of churn mentions "poor support experience"  
- 8% of churn mentions "sales tools lacking"

## Recommendation
Export feature is table stakes now. Support integration is emerging as differentiator. Sales notes is "nice to have" but not driving decisions.
