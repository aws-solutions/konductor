# Support Ticket Analysis - Q2 2025

**From:** Maria Rodriguez, Support Lead  
**To:** Product Team  
**Date:** June 5, 2025

## Executive Summary
Support ticket volume increased 22% YoY in Q2, primarily driven by calendar synchronization issues and permission management. Our top pain point: customers struggling to track support interactions across their organization.

## Top Support Issues (by volume)

1. **Calendar sync failures**: 320 tickets (28%)
2. **Permission confusion**: 285 tickets (25%)  
3. **Support history tracking**: 195 tickets (17%)
4. **API integration issues**: 155 tickets (14%)
5. **UI/UX complaints**: 115 tickets (10%)
6. **Other**: 65 tickets (6%)

**Total**: 1,135 tickets

## "Support Tickets" Feature Request Analysis
Customers have requested better support ticket integration 87 times in Q2. The ask: automatically create support tickets from calendar events and track resolution status.

### Impact Assessment
- Could reduce "support history tracking" tickets by ~70% (136 tickets)
- Would address 17% of total support volume
- Estimated support cost reduction: $18,500/month
- Customer satisfaction (CSAT) improvement: +15 points

## Customer Quotes
> "We waste hours every week trying to remember which support rep we talked to about which calendar issue." - TechStart Inc
> "If support tickets were linked to calendar events, our whole team could see the status without asking me." - DataFlow Co
> "This would cut our internal coordination time in half." - Acme Corp

## Strategic Alignment
Directly supports Q3 goal of "Reduce support ticket volume by 20%" and "Improve operational efficiency". Also supports enterprise readiness by providing better visibility into support workflows.
