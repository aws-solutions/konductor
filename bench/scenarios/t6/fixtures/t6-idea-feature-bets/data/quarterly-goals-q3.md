# Quarterly Goals - Q3 2025

**From:** Priya Sharma, Head of Product  
**To:** Product Team  
**Date:** June 15, 2025

## Strategic Focus Areas for Q3

1. **Improve Customer Retention**
   - Reduce churn from current 8% to 6%
   - Address top reasons for cancellation
   - Focus on enterprise customers (they have highest LTV)

2. **Enterprise Readiness**
   - Features that make us more attractive to large organizations
   - Better integration with existing workflows
   - Support for complex organizational structures

3. **Operational Efficiency**  
   - Reduce support ticket volume by 20%
   - Automate common customer requests
   - Improve self-service capabilities

## Success Metrics
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR): Target 115%
- Enterprise segment growth: +25% revenue
- Support cost per customer: Reduce by 15%

## Notes from Leadership Offsite
The board is concerned about our support costs growing faster than revenue. Any feature that helps reduce support burden gets extra points. Also, we need to show progress on enterprise features for the Series B pitch deck.
