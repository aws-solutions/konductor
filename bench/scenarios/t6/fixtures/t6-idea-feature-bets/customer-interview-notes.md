# Customer Interview Notes - May 2025

**Interviewer:** Jamie Lee, Product Researcher  
**Dates:** May 10-25, 2025

## Participants
12 customers (mix of SMB, mid-market, enterprise)

## Key Themes

### Export CSV Feature
- "We need to export our calendar data for compliance reporting" - Finance team at Acme Corp
- "Monthly reports to leadership require customer IDs tied to calendar usage" - TechStart Inc
- "Current export is missing metadata we need" - 8/12 customers mentioned this

### Sales Notes Feature  
- "Sales team lives in calendars but notes are scattered" - Sales Director, DataFlow Co
- "Would save 2-3 hours per rep per week" - Multiple sales managers
- "Important for enterprise deals where multiple people interact with client"

### Support Tickets Feature
- "Biggest pain point is tracking issues over time" - Support Manager, CloudNine Ltd
- "When calendar sync fails, we need to create ticket, but then lose context" - IT Admin, SwiftSoft
- "Enterprise customers need audit trails" - Multiple enterprise contacts

## Prioritization Signals
- Export CSV: High urgency for compliance teams
- Sales Notes: High value for revenue teams  
- Support Tickets: High pain relief for operations teams

## Interesting Note
Acme Corp mentioned export feature in EVERY interview session (5 separate interviews). Their compliance team seems very vocal.
