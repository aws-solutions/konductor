# Financial Impact Summary - Feature Analysis

**Prepared by:** Finance Team  
**Date:** June 14, 2025

## Assumptions
- Feature development cost: $75K (engineering time)
- Support cost per ticket: $85
- Customer lifetime value: 24 months average

- **Retention impact:** Could reduce churn by 1% (worth ~$45K/year)
- **Cost savings:** None
- **Net impact:** ~$15K/year positive (after dev cost)
