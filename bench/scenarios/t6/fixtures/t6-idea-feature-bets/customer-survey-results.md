# Customer Survey Results - Feature Prioritization

**Survey period:** May 2025  
**Respondents:** 89 customers (59% response rate)

## Question: "Which feature would be most valuable to you?"

1. **Export CSV with customer IDs**: 42 votes (47%)
2. **Sales notes in calendar**: 28 votes (31%)
3. **Support ticket integration**: 19 votes (21%)

## Comments Analysis

### Export CSV (42 comments)
- "Need for audit trails" (12 mentions)
- "Compliance reporting requirement" (9 mentions)
- "Monthly leadership reports" (8 mentions)
- "Acme Corp team mentioned this is critical" (3 mentions)

### Sales Notes (28 comments)
- "Would save sales team time" (15 mentions)
- "Better client tracking" (9 mentions)
- "Enterprise deals need this" (4 mentions)

### Support Tickets (19 comments)
- "Would reduce internal coordination" (11 mentions)
- "Support visibility important" (6 mentions)
- "Enterprise feature" (2 mentions)

## Demographic Breakdown
- **Enterprise customers:** 65% prefer export, 25% sales notes, 10% support tickets
- **Mid-market:** 40% export, 40% sales notes, 20% support tickets  
- **SMB:** 35% export, 30% sales notes, 35% support tickets

## Note
Survey may be skewed by Acme Corp encouraging their team to vote for export feature.
