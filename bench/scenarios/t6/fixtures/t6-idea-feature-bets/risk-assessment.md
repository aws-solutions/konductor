# Risk Assessment - Feature Development

**Prepared by:** Product & Engineering  
**Date:** June 16, 2025

## Export CSV Feature

### Technical Risks: Low
- Data joins could impact performance
- GDPR compliance requires careful handling

### Business Risks: Medium  
- One customer (Acme Corp) driving majority of demand
- If they churn, feature value drops significantly
- Competitors already have this feature

### Market Risks: Low
- Table stakes feature
- No differentiation value

## Sales Notes Feature

### Technical Risks: Medium
- Sync across devices complex
- Rich text editor integration challenges

### Business Risks: High
- Revenue projections may be inflated
- Double counting detected in preliminary review
- Sales team historically optimistic

### Market Risks: Medium
- Competitors have basic versions
- Not a primary purchase driver

## Support Tickets Feature

### Technical Risks: High
- Multiple external API integrations
- Error handling complex
- Support system compatibility issues

### Business Risks: Low
- Clear cost savings measurable
- Aligns with strategic goals
- Addresses board concerns

### Market Risks: Low  
- Emerging differentiator
- Competitor (SyncCalendar) winning with this

## Overall Risk Ranking
1. Support Tickets (high tech risk, low business risk)
2. Sales Notes (medium tech risk, high business risk)  
3. Export CSV (low tech risk, medium business risk)
