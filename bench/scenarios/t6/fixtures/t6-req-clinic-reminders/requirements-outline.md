# Reminder project outline, version 0.2

Goal: reduce avoidable missed visits without making the front desk responsible for another manual list.

Possible scope: appointment reminders, delivery status, a way to stop messages, and a basic outcome report. Out of scope for this outline: billing notices, clinical follow-up, marketing, and two-way scheduling.

Open items: copy, timing, language, consent rule, clinic sequence, reply owner, pause control, and retention. The outline was left unfinished after the vendor demo.
