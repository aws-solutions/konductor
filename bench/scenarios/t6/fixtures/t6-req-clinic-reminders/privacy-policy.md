# Patient communications policy excerpt

Policy owner: Noor Haddad, compliance
Published 2023-06-01; review stamp 2024-08-20

The clinic may use contact information to communicate about care, scheduling, billing, and access needs when a patient has provided an appropriate contact preference. Staff should use the least amount of information needed for the purpose and should verify identity before discussing care details.

Text messages can be seen by anyone with access to the device or number. Staff must not put clinical findings, diagnoses, or detailed treatment information in a general text. The policy does not provide a prescribed appointment-reminder template.

Patients may change a communication preference through the front desk or the patient portal. Staff must record the change in the patient record. The excerpt does not define an automated keyword, a response workflow, or a clinic-specific quiet period.
