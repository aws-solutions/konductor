# Front-desk interview: Cedar Grove

Interviewee: Lena Ortiz, senior receptionist
Date: 2024-11-14

Lena says the desk is busiest between 7:30 and 9:00 in the morning. She does not want a new alert for every changed appointment. “If it is another screen, it will be ignored,” she said.

Cedar Grove has a lot of older patients who ask family members to help with phones. Some patients give a home number, some give a mobile number, and a few share one number between two people. Lena writes “text ok” in free text when the consent checkbox is missing, so that note is not in the export.

She would like the reminder to make it obvious which location is involved. She was unsure whether a therapist's name or the type of exercise visit can be included. Patients already call the main line when they are confused.
