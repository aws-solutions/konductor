# Current booking process

Owner: Priya Nair, operations coordinator
Last refreshed: 2024-10-28

1. A patient calls or uses the web form. The front desk creates the appointment in the booking system.
2. The desk confirms the mobile number and records whether the patient agreed to text contact. The consent field is sometimes left blank on old records.
3. The desk sends a manually edited confirmation when the booking is made. There is no standard reminder template.
4. Reschedules are entered as a new appointment after the old slot is cancelled. Front-desk staff check the schedule at the beginning of each shift.
5. On the morning after a missed appointment, the clinic exports a no-show list and calls selected patients. There is no common rule for selecting them.

A reminder must never be treated as a substitute for the booking system. The booking system is the source for appointment status, but staff occasionally print a paper list for the day.

Known loose ends from the refresh:
- The exports do not agree on whether a cancelled visit remains in the appointment list.
- The clinic directory has local times while the vendor examples use UTC.
- The team has not agreed what a patient should see in a text or where a reply should go.
