# Front-desk interview: Harbor Point and Northgate

Interviewees: Marcus Bell (Harbor Point), June Park (Northgate)
Date: 2024-11-19

Marcus says Harbor Point often books follow-up visits several days in advance, while Northgate has more same-week bookings. June says Northgate's patients sometimes arrive early because the printed directions do not show the entrance clearly.

Both desks said the clinic should be named in any patient-facing notice. Neither desk has a shared inbox for texts today. Replies currently go wherever the sending number happens to be configured, and staff are not sure who checks it.

June keeps a paper list of people who ask not to be texted. Marcus said that list is not reconciled with the booking system every day. They both asked whether a patient can stop messages without speaking to staff.

Questions written in the margin: “Do we need translated copy? What if two appointments have the same number? Can the vendor handle a number that is landline-only?”
