# Staff Interview - Circulation Desk Workers

**Date:** December 10, 2023  
**Participants:** Sarah (Central), Mike (Westside), Lisa (Eastside), Jamal (North Hill)  
**Facilitator:** Mark (PM)

## Current Pain Points

### Phone Hold System
- "We get 50+ calls per hour during peak times" - Sarah
- "Average call takes 8 minutes, most of that is looking up availability" - Mike
- "Patrons get frustrated waiting on hold" - Lisa

### Manual Processes
- Writing hold slips by hand
- Tracking waitlists on paper or spreadsheets
- Calling patrons when items arrive (takes 2-3 minutes per call)
- No central view of all holds across branches

### Special Cases
- Institutional cards: Need manager approval for holds over 10 items
- Children's cards: Parents want notification when their child's hold is ready
- Senior patrons: Some prefer phone calls over digital notifications
- Patrons with visual impairments: Current system doesn't work well with screen readers

## Desired Features in New System

### Must Have
1. Real-time inventory lookup
2. Automated phone and email notifications
3. Waitlist management
4. Branch transfer tracking
5. Simple interface for staff overrides

### Should Have
1. Text message notifications
2. Mobile app for staff
3. Bulk hold operations
4. Analytics dashboard
5. Integration with fine system

### Could Have
1. Predictive wait time estimates
2. Patron preference profiles
3. Social sharing of reading lists
4. Recommendation engine
5. Reading challenge integration

## Concerns
- "Will it work during internet outages?" - Jamal
- "How will we train patrons who aren't tech-savvy?" - Lisa
- "What about patrons without email or smartphones?" - Mike
- "Will it increase our workload during transition?" - Sarah

## Notes
- Mike mentioned that Westside branch has special arrangement with local school - students get priority during exam periods (needs verification)
- Lisa noted that Eastside has limited storage space - can only hold 50 items at once (contradicts branches.md which says 75)
- Sarah suggested having a "quick pickup" area near entrance for popular holds
- Jamal concerned about North Hill's limited hours - patrons might miss pickup windows

## Action Items
1. Verify branch storage capacities
2. Research notification preferences by demographic
3. Develop training plan for staff and patrons
4. Create contingency plans for system outages
