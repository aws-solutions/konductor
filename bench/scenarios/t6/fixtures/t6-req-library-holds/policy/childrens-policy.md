# Children's Library Services Policy

## Card Eligibility
- Children age 0-11: Juvenile card (parent/guardian signature required)
- Teens age 12-17: Young Adult card (may apply independently in some cases)
- Age verification: Birth certificate, school ID, or parent statement

## Hold Limits
- Juvenile cards (0-11): Maximum 5 items on hold at once
- Young Adult cards (12-17): Maximum 10 items on hold at once
- **Exception**: Summer reading program participants get +2 additional holds

## Parental Controls
- Parents may link to child's account for management
- Parent receives notifications for child's holds
- Parent can place holds on child's behalf
- Child cannot exceed limits without parent override

## Content Restrictions
- Children's cards cannot place holds on adult (18+) materials
- Young Adult cards may access teen and adult collections
- Parent may request restrictions on specific content types

## Privacy Considerations
- COPPA compliance required for online accounts
- Parent consent required for data collection under age 13
- Separate communication preferences for parent and child
- Child's reading history confidential from other patrons

## Special Programs
- Summer Reading: June-August, extended hold limits
- School Partnerships: Teacher cards for classroom use
- Homeschool Families: Special consideration for bulk holds
- Storytime Groups: Priority holds for program materials

## Safety
- Children under 8 must be accompanied by adult for pickup
- Staff verification of parent/child relationship for sensitive holds
- Secure pickup process for high-value items
- Lost card replacement: Parent must request in person

## Digital Access
- Children's e-books: Unlimited simultaneous access model
- Parental controls for internet access on library computers
- Screen time recommendations displayed for digital materials
- Educational content prioritized in recommendations

## Notes
- Policy aligns with ALA guidelines for youth services
- Regular review with child development experts
- Training for staff on youth privacy laws
- Partnership with local schools for literacy initiatives
