# County Library Service - Circulation Policy (Excerpt)

## Hold Policies

### General Rules
- All library card holders may place holds on available items
- Holds can be placed online, by phone, or in person
- Maximum holds per card: 15 items (under review)
- Maximum holds per card for children (under 12): 5 items
- Maximum holds per card for teens (13-17): 10 items

### Pickup and Expiration
- Patrons have 7 days to pick up held items once notified
- Items not picked up within the pickup window are returned to circulation
- Patrons may renew holds once for an additional 3 days if needed
- After a hold expires, the item goes to the next patron in the waitlist

### Fines and Fees
- No fines for expired holds (policy change effective March 1, 2024)
- Previously: $0.50 per day fine for not picking up holds
- Replacement fees still apply for lost or damaged items

### Special Collections
- Reference materials and archival items cannot be placed on hold
- New release books have a 2-week lending period with no renewals
- Popular titles may have maximum hold limits during peak seasons

### Card Types
- Regular cards: Standard privileges
- Student cards: Additional 5 hold limit during school year
- Senior cards (65+): Extended pickup window (10 days)
- Institutional cards: Special handling required, contact branch
