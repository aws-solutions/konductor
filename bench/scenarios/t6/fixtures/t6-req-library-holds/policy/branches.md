# Library Branch Information

   - Services: Full catalog, inter-library loans, e-book support
   - Contact: (555) 123-4567

2. Westside Branch
   - Address: 456 Oak Ave
   - Hours: Mon-Fri 10am-8pm, Sat 10am-4pm, Sun Closed

   - Contact: (555) 234-5678

3. Eastside Branch
   - Address: 789 Pine Blvd
   - Hours: Tue-Sat 10am-6pm, Sun-Mon Closed

   - Contact: (555) 345-6789

## Special Notes for Holds System
- All branches except North Hill support e-book holds
- Inter-library holds available only at Central and Westside
- Bookmobile stops: Schedule varies, check website
- Holiday closures: All branches closed on federal holidays

## Branch Capacities
- Central: Can store up to 200 holds at once
- Westside: Can store up to 150 holds at once
- Eastside: Can store up to 75 holds at once
- North Hill: Can store up to 50 holds at once
- Riverside: Can store up to 40 holds at once (temporary)
