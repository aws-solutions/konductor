# Board Memorandum

**To:** Library Services Committee  
**From:** Director of Library Services  
**Date:** January 5, 2024  
**Subject:** New Holds System - Strategic Direction and Constraints

## Executive Summary
The board has approved funding for the new online holds and waitlist system with the following strategic objectives and constraints.

## Budget Approval
- Total project budget: $350,000
- Implementation timeline: 12 months
- Operating budget impact: $45,000 annually
- Funding source: Technology Improvement Grant (85%), County funds (15%)

## Strategic Objectives
1. **Patron Experience**: Reduce hold placement time from average 8 minutes to under 2 minutes
2. **Accessibility**: Achieve WCAG 2.1 AA compliance (non-negotiable)
3. **Integration**: Seamless connection with existing ILS (LibraryWorld v7.2)
4. **Scalability**: Support 50,000+ active patrons
5. **Mobile First**: Optimize for smartphone access

## Policy Directives

### 1. Equity and Access
- No branch may be excluded from the initial launch
- **Exception**: Bookmobile services will be Phase 2 (Q3 2024)
- All card types must have equal access to core features
- Special accommodations for patrons with disabilities required

### 2. Financial Constraints
- No per-transaction fees allowed in vendor contracts
- Annual operating costs capped at $50,000
- Must use existing county IT infrastructure where possible
- Cloud hosting acceptable but must meet data sovereignty requirements

### 3. Implementation Phases
- **Phase 1 (Q2 2024)**: Basic holds functionality at all branches
- **Phase 2 (Q3 2024)**: Waitlist management and notifications
- **Phase 3 (Q4 2024)**: Mobile app and advanced features
- **Phase 4 (Q1 2025)**: Analytics and reporting dashboard

### 4. Special Considerations
- **Children's privacy**: COPPA compliance mandatory for under-13 accounts
- **Data retention**: Hold history limited to 18 months maximum
- **Notification preferences**: Must include opt-out for all marketing messages
- **Emergency overrides**: System must allow manual hold management during outages

## Success Metrics
- 80% of holds placed online within 6 months of launch
- 95% system uptime requirement
- Patron satisfaction score target: 4.2/5.0
- Staff training completion: 100% of frontline staff

## Next Steps
1. Finalize requirements document by January 31
2. Issue RFP to pre-qualified vendors by February 15
3. Vendor selection by March 31
4. Begin implementation April 15
