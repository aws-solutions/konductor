# Support triage — 10 February 2025

Reva reviewed 214 onboarding contacts from the last five weeks. The tagging is manual and not every contact includes a provider.

- “Email never arrived” grew from single digits in early January to 64 tickets in the week beginning 27 January.
- Several people said an alternate address worked. One person forwarded a Cloudmail rejection notice; support did not verify its authenticity.
- Only 8 tickets in the last week mention confusion about profile categories.
- A handful of contacts were duplicate tickets, and the tag counts should not be treated as unique people.

Suggested follow-up: capture the provider and delivery status in the contact form. Reva also wrote “maybe add resend?” in the margin. This is an operational idea, not a tested product solution.
