# First-run observations — 9 January 2025

Observer: Jules Neri. Five people tried the pre-4.19.0 build in the small lab.

People understood the value of a saved plan after the preview. Two people paused at the category examples, but both continued without help. The verification message arrived before the facilitator moved to the next task for all five sessions.

The sample was recruited from existing users and is too small for a rate estimate. It says little about inbox delivery outside the lab.
