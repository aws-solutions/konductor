# Privacy notice excerpt

Northstar processes contact details, scheduling information, and information entered into appointment forms to provide the service. Customers may manage people and records in the console. Northstar does not display secret credentials or access tokens in the console.

The notice describes access, correction, and portability rights in general terms. It does not define the product's requester verification flow, archive format, delivery service level, or how a download link should expire. A portability response must avoid exposing information that belongs only to Northstar's internal operations or another customer.

Reviewed by Leena Ortiz, Privacy, 2024-09-19. This excerpt is not a complete implementation specification.
