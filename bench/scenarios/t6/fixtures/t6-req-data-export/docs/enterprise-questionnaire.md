# Acme Harbor security questionnaire excerpt

Customer: Acme Harbor, renewal review
Prepared by: Jo Bell, Customer Assurance
Date: 2025-02-24

- Do customers receive a copy of information associated with their tenant? Current answer: “Requests are handled through Support; product flow is being evaluated.”
- Are exports encrypted in transit and at rest? Current answer: “The platform uses the standard object storage controls.”
- Can an administrator download a complete tenant copy? Current answer: “The permission model is under review.”
- Are export requests and downloads auditable? Current answer: “Activity is retained by the platform, but event names and retention are not documented here.”
- Are archives shared with any other tenant? The service is designed to isolate tenants; the export behavior itself has not been specified.

Do not treat this questionnaire as a product commitment. Acme Harbor asked for a clear answer before its next review.
