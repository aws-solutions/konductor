# Records and deletion handbook excerpt

Operational records are classified separately from customer content. Customer content follows account deletion workflows, while service diagnostics and security events follow their own schedules. A deletion request can be in progress while an appointment export is being assembled.

The handbook does not say whether a completed export is a customer record, how long a generated package may remain available, or whether a new request is needed after a download link is no longer valid.
