# Communication Plan

## Regular Meetings
- **Daily Standup:** 10:15am, 15 minutes
- **Sprint Planning:** Every other Monday, 2 hours
- **Sprint Review:** End of sprint, 1 hour
- **Retrospective:** End of sprint, 1 hour
- **Steering Committee:** Monthly, 1 hour

## Reporting
- **Weekly Status Report:** Every Friday to stakeholders
- **Burndown Chart:** Updated daily
- **Risk Register:** Updated weekly
- **Budget Report:** Monthly

## Communication Channels
- **Slack:** Team coordination, quick questions
- **Email:** Formal communications, decisions
- **Confluence:** Documentation, meeting notes
- **Jira:** Task tracking, backlog

## Escalation Path
1. Team member → Tech Lead
2. Tech Lead → Project Manager
3. Project Manager → VP of Product
4. VP of Product → Steering Committee

## Stakeholder Updates
- Linda Thompson: Weekly summary
- Metro Housing Group: Bi-weekly progress report
- Tenant Advisory Board: Monthly feature preview
