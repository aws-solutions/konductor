# Contract Excerpt - Metro Housing Group Agreement

## Section 4.2: Compliance Reporting Requirements

4.2.1 The Tenant Portal shall include automated compliance reporting functionality that tracks:
(a) Maintenance request response times (within 24 hours for emergency, 72 hours for routine)
(b) Rent payment processing accuracy (99.9% target)
(c) Document retention compliance (7-year archive)

4.2.2 Compliance reports must be accessible to Metro Housing Group property managers via a dedicated dashboard interface.

4.2.3 The system must generate monthly compliance summaries by the 5th business day of each following month.

## Section 7.1: Launch Obligations

7.1.1 Oakwood Properties agrees to deliver a functional Tenant Portal MVP by April 15, 2025.

7.1.2 Failure to meet this deadline will result in liquidated damages of $15,000 per week of delay, up to a maximum of $75,000.

7.1.3 The MVP must include at minimum:
- Tenant authentication and profile management
- Maintenance request submission and tracking
- Rent payment processing
- Compliance reporting as defined in Section 4.2

## Appendix B: Priority Features
The following features are classified as "Must Have" for the MVP:
1. Tenant authentication system
2. Maintenance request workflow
3. Rent payment processing
4. Compliance reporting dashboard
