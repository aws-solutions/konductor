MEMORANDUM

To: Tenant Portal Project Team
From: Linda Thompson, VP of Product
Date: December 15, 2024
Subject: MVP Launch Date Confirmation

After discussions with the executive team and our legal department, I'm writing to confirm the launch timeline for the Tenant Portal MVP.

## Key Decision
The MVP must launch by **April 15, 2025**. This date is non-negotiable due to:

1. Contractual obligations with our enterprise client, Metro Housing Group
2. Q2 financial reporting requirements
3. Marketing campaign already scheduled for mid-April

## Implications
- We have approximately 14 weeks of development time from January 6 to April 15
- The team will work at full capacity (no additional hiring planned)
- Scope must be carefully managed to meet this deadline

## Success Criteria
The MVP must include:
- Core tenant authentication and profile management
- Basic maintenance request functionality  
- Rent payment processing
- Property manager dashboard
- Contractual compliance features (per our agreement)

Please work with the product team to define what can realistically be delivered within this timeframe. We need a feasible plan by January 10.
