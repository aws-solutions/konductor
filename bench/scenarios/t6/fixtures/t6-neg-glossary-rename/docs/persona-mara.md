# Persona: Mara, the independent researcher

Mara works from a rented room and keeps a narrow desk workspace beside the window. She dislikes tools that make her create a team before she can write anything. On busy days she captures fragments on her phone and sorts them into a Workspace on Sunday.

She cares about fast capture, readable exports, and knowing who can see a note. She does not care about project-management language. Her interview was short and a little contradictory: she wants fewer prompts, but asked for a prompt that reminds her to add sources.
