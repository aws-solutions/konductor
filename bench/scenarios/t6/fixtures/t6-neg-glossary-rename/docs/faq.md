# Frequently asked questions

## Can I use Pinboard alone?

Yes. A personal Workspace does not require invitations. You can share it later if a project grows.

## How do I invite someone?

Open the Workspace menu, choose Members, and enter an email address. The invitee can accept from the email or from the notifications panel.

## What happens when I leave a Workspace?

Your notes remain with the Workspace unless you created a personal copy first. Ask an owner to invite you again if you left by mistake.

## Does deleting a note delete its attachments?

Attachments are removed with the note after the recovery period expires. Export anything you need first.
