# Privacy handbook excerpt

Notes can contain personal or confidential information. Pinboard staff must not open a customer's note unless the customer has requested support and the case requires it. Screenshots in an internal ticket should hide names, email addresses, and note text that is not needed to reproduce the issue.

Workspace membership is customer-controlled. A support agent may confirm membership state but must not add themselves to a customer's Workspace for convenience.

Report suspected exposure to the privacy lead and preserve the case reference. Do not paste raw exports into chat.
