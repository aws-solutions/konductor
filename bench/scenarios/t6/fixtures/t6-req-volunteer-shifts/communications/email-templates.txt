# Email Templates for Volunteer Communications

## 1. Shift Confirmation
Subject: Your Community Food Hub shift is confirmed!

Hi [Volunteer Name],

Thank you for signing up to volunteer!

**Shift Details:**
- Date: [Date]
- Time: [Start Time] - [End Time] [time zone?]
- Site: [Site Name]
- Role: [Role]
- Address: [Site Address]

**What to bring:**
- Closed-toe shoes
- Water bottle
- Weather-appropriate clothing

**Reminders:**
- Please arrive 10 minutes early for check-in
- Contact [Site Coordinator] at [Phone] if running late
- Cancellations require 24 hours notice

We appreciate your support!

The Community Food Hub Team

**When:** [Date], [Start Time] - [End Time] [time zone?]

## 4. Cancellation Notice
Subject: Your shift has been cancelled

Hi [Volunteer Name],

Your shift on [Date] at [Site Name] has been cancelled.

**Reason:** [Reason - optional]

We appreciate you letting us know in advance. Please sign up for another shift when you're available.

Thank you for your understanding.

## 5. Background Check Required
Subject: Action required: Background check for volunteer role

Hi [Volunteer Name],

The [Role] role you've signed up for requires a background check.

**Next steps:**
1. Complete the authorization form: [Link]
2. We'll initiate the background check
3. You'll be notified when cleared (processing time varies)

You cannot volunteer in this role until the background check is complete.

Please complete the authorization within 48 hours.

## Notes:
- Personalize with volunteer/site coordinator names
- Include emergency contact info for site
- Add site-specific instructions when needed
- Consider SMS alternatives for time-sensitive reminders
