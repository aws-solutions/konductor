# Community Food Hub Volunteer Newsletter - March 2024

## Exciting News: New Digital Sign-up System Coming!

We're thrilled to announce that we're developing a new digital volunteer sign-up system to replace our current spreadsheet. The new system will launch in **June 2024** and will make signing up for shifts easier than ever!

### What to Expect:
- **Mobile-friendly interface** - sign up from your phone or tablet
- **Automated reminders** - never forget a shift again
- **Real-time availability** - see which shifts need volunteers
- **Group booking** - volunteer with friends, family, or coworkers
- **Multi-language support** - starting with English and Spanish

### Volunteer Spotlight: Maria Garcia
Maria has volunteered with us for over a year and recently helped translate our safety materials into Spanish. "I love being able to use my language skills to help our community," she says.

### Upcoming Events
- **April 15:** Spring Food Drive - extra volunteers needed!
- **April 22:** Earth Day Community Clean-up
- **May 5-11:** National Volunteer Week celebrations

### Important Reminders
- **Background checks:** Drivers and kitchen staff must have current background checks on file
- **Training:** New shelter volunteers need to complete safeguarding training
- **Group volunteers:** Book at least 2 weeks in advance
- **Cancellations:** Please give 24 hours notice if you can't make your shift

### We Need Your Help!
We're looking for volunteers to help test the new sign-up system in May. If you're interested, email Sarah at sarah.j@communityfoodhub.org.

**Stay tuned for more updates!**
