# Volunteer Interview Notes - March 2024

## Interview with Maria Garcia (VOL-002)
**Role:** Sorting volunteer, bilingual (English/Spanish)
**Experience:** 1 year, 35 shifts completed

**Feedback:**
- Likes volunteering but finds spreadsheet confusing
- Would prefer Spanish interface option
- Concerned about privacy - doesn't want personal info visible to all
- Suggests buddy system for new volunteers
- Works night shifts - can't do early morning reminders

## Interview with David Kim (VOL-005)

**Feedback:**

## Interview with Patricia Hall (VOL-012)

## Common Themes:
1. Mobile access needed
2. Privacy concerns
3. Reminder system critical
4. Group volunteering popular
5. Language support needed (Spanish)
6. Age verification/consent process clunky
7. Background check delays problematic
8. Time zone confusion for remote helpers
