# Shift Patterns Analysis
**Analyst:** Miguel Rodriguez
**Date:** March 10, 2024

## Data Source
- volunteer-shifts-2024.csv (24 shifts, March 15-20)
- volunteer-demographics.csv (26 volunteers)
- sites.csv (6 sites)

## Key Findings

### 1. Shift Fill Rates
- Downtown Distribution Center: 92% (high demand)
- Westside Community Center: 88% 
- Northgate Shelter: 75% (lower due to special requirements)
- Eastside Church: 83%
- South Park Center: 85%
- Riverside Commons: 88%

### 2. Role Distribution
- Packing: 42% of shifts
- Sorting: 33% of shifts  
- Driver: 17% of shifts (often unfilled due to background checks)
- Kitchen Assistant: 4% of shifts
- Distribution: 4% of shifts

### 3. Time Preferences
- Morning shifts (8am-12pm): 58% fill rate
- Afternoon shifts (12pm-4pm): 67% fill rate
- Late afternoon (4pm-6pm): 42% fill rate

### 4. Volunteer Frequency
- 35% of volunteers do 1 shift per month
- 45% do 2-3 shifts per month
- 15% do 4+ shifts per month
- 5% are new (0-1 month)

### 5. Age Distribution
- 16-17: 8% of volunteers
- 18-25: 23% of volunteers
- 26-45: 35% of volunteers
- 46-65: 27% of volunteers
- 65+: 8% of volunteers

### 6. Language Needs
- English only: 77%
- English + Spanish: 15%
- English + Other: 8%

## Operational Implications

### 1. Shift Caps May Be Too Restrictive
Some popular sites (Downtown) could support more volunteers per shift. Current caps based on physical space, not demand.

### 2. Driver Shortage Critical
17% of driver shifts unfilled. Background check processing time is a barrier.

### 3. Age Distribution Healthy
Good mix across ages, but need to ensure senior volunteers have appropriate roles.

### 4. Language Support Justified
15% Spanish speakers confirms need for bilingual interface.

### 5. Time Zone Confusion Observed
In survey, 3 volunteers mentioned confusion about "Mountain Time vs Arizona time."

## Recommendations

### Short-term (System Design)
1. Implement flexible shift caps based on role mix
2. Streamline background check process for drivers
3. Resolve time zone confusion for remote volunteers
4. Spanish interface as priority

### Medium-term (Operations)
1. Recruit more drivers
2. Create "driver trainee" role with supervision
3. Develop role-specific training materials
4. Consider shift timing adjustments based on demand

### Long-term (Strategy)
1. Analyze seasonal patterns (summer vs winter)
2. Develop volunteer retention program
3. Expand to evening/weekend shifts if demand exists
4. Partner with local businesses for group volunteering

## Data Gaps
1. No data on why volunteers cancel
2. Limited data on volunteer satisfaction
3. No tracking of volunteer referrals
4. Seasonal patterns not captured (only March data)
