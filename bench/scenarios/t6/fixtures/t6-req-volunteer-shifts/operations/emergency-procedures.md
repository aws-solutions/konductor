# Emergency Procedures for Volunteer Sites

## 1. Emergency Contacts
**Primary:** Site Coordinator (on-site during shifts)
**Secondary:** Volunteer Coordinator - Sarah Johnson (555-1001)
**Safety Officer:** Dr. Alisha Chen (555-1003)
**Medical Emergency:** 911

## 2. Communication Chain
1. Site coordinator assesses situation
2. Notifies emergency services if needed (911)
3. Contacts Volunteer Coordinator
4. Volunteer Coordinator notifies Safety Officer
5. Family/emergency contacts notified as appropriate

## 3. Volunteer Information Requirements
All volunteers must provide:
- Emergency contact name and phone
- Any medical conditions affecting emergency response
- Allergy information (if relevant to site)
- Current medications (voluntary disclosure)

## 4. Site-Specific Risks

### Downtown Distribution Center
- Forklift operation (certified operators only)
- Refrigerated storage (lock-out/tag-out procedures)
- Loading dock safety

### Westside Community Center
- Stair access to basement storage
- Limited parking lot lighting
- Shared facility with other groups

### Northgate Shelter
- Vulnerable population protocols
- Kitchen safety (burn prevention)
- Conflict de-escalation procedures

### Eastside Church
- Basement access (wheelchair ramp available)
- Shared parking with church events
- Food storage in commercial kitchen

### South Park Center
- Outdoor distribution (weather considerations)
- Park district facility rules
- Portable toilet facilities

### Riverside Commons
- New site - procedures still being developed
- Construction areas marked
- Temporary fencing in place

## 5. Incident Reporting
All incidents must be reported within 24 hours using Incident Report Form (IRF-001).

**Reportable incidents:**
- Injuries requiring first aid or medical attention
- Property damage over $100
- Safeguarding concerns
- Volunteer conflicts
- Safety violations

## 6. First Aid Requirements
Each site must have:
- Fully stocked first aid kit
- AED (where available)
- Trained first aid responder (at least one per shift)
- Emergency evacuation map posted

## 7. Weather Cancellations
**Criteria for shift cancellation:**
- Severe weather warning (National Weather Service)
- Temperature below 10°F or above 100°F
- Air quality index over 150 (unhealthy)
- Road closures affecting site access

**Cancellation process:**
1. Decision by 6pm day before shift
2. Email and SMS notification to affected volunteers
3. Site coordinator confirms building closure if applicable
4. Rescheduling options provided

## 8. Volunteer Safety Training
**Required for all volunteers:**
- Site orientation (first shift)
- Emergency evacuation procedures
- Basic first aid awareness
- Reporting procedures

**Role-specific training:**
- Drivers: defensive driving course
- Kitchen staff: food safety certification
- Coordinators: incident management training

## 9. Data in Emergency
**Information needed immediately:**
- Volunteer roster for current shift
- Emergency contacts
- Medical information (if provided)
- Site coordinator contact

**System requirement:** Emergency access mode for coordinators to retrieve critical information quickly.
