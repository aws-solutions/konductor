# Volunteer Handbook (Excerpt)

## Chapter 3: Shift Management

### 3.1 Sign-up Procedures
Volunteers can sign up for shifts through the current spreadsheet system. Shifts are released on a monthly basis.

**Key rules:**
- Maximum 3 shifts per week per volunteer
- Shifts must be claimed at least 48 hours in advance
- Cancellations require 24 hours notice
- No-shows: 2 incidents result in temporary suspension

### 3.2 Shift Caps
To ensure fair distribution of opportunities, each site has capacity limits based on space and safety.

### 3.3 Role Requirements

**Driver Role:**
- Valid driver's license
- Clean driving record (last 3 years)
- Vehicle insurance meeting state minimums
- Background check completion

**Kitchen Assistant:**
- Food handler certification (or willingness to obtain)
- Additional safeguarding training for shelter sites

**Distribution Roles:**
- Ability to lift 25 lbs
- Customer service training recommended

### 3.4 Group Bookings
Community groups, corporate teams, and school groups may book shifts. Advance booking and group coordinator orientation required.

### 3.5 Communication
- Shift reminders sent 48 hours and 24 hours before shift
- Weather cancellations communicated by 6pm the day before
- Site-specific instructions provided upon sign-up

## Chapter 4: Volunteer Rights and Responsibilities

### 4.1 Privacy
Volunteers have the right to:
- Confidential handling of personal information
- Opt-out of photos/videos for promotional materials
- Control over contact preferences

### 4.2 Data Access
Site coordinators can view:
- Volunteer name and contact information for their site
- Shift history for their site
- Emergency contact information

Site coordinators **cannot** view certain sensitive information for privacy and safeguarding reasons.
