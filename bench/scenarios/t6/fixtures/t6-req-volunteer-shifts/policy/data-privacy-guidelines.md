# Data Privacy Guidelines for Volunteer Management System

## 1. Data Classification

### 1.1 Public Information
- Volunteer first name and last initial (e.g., "John S.")
- Total volunteer hours (aggregate)
- Site assignments (without specific dates/times)

### 1.2 Internal Information
- Full name and contact details
- Shift history
- Skills and qualifications
- Emergency contact information

### 1.3 Confidential Information
- Age verification information
- Background check results
- Medical information (if provided)
- Performance evaluations
- Disciplinary actions

### 1.4 Restricted Information
- Social Security numbers (for background checks only)
- Driver's license numbers
- Bank information (for reimbursement)

## 2. Access Controls

### 2.1 Volunteer Access
- Can view own profile and shift history
- Cannot view other volunteers' personal information
- Can see other volunteers signed up for same shift (name only)

### 2.2 Site Coordinator Access
- Can view volunteer information for their site only
- Cannot view age, background check details, or medical information
- Can see emergency contacts for safety purposes

### 2.3 Volunteer Coordinator Access
- Full access to all volunteer data
- Background check administration
- Performance management

### 2.4 IT Administrator Access
- Technical access only (no routine viewing of volunteer data)
- Access logged and audited

## 3. Data Retention

### 3.1 Active Volunteers
- All data retained while volunteer is active
- Inactive after 12 months with no shifts

### 3.2 Inactive Volunteers
- Basic profile retained for 3 years
- Shift history archived after 1 year
- Contact information anonymized after 2 years

### 3.3 Background Checks
- Results retained for duration of volunteer service
- Destroyed 30 days after volunteer becomes inactive
- Summary retained (pass/fail, date) for 3 years

## 4. Consent Management

### 4.1 Required Consents
- Privacy policy acceptance
- Photo release (optional)
- Emergency contact information
- Background check authorization
- Parental consent for volunteers under 18

### 4.2 Consent Storage
- Digital signatures with timestamp
- Version tracking for policy updates
- Easy withdrawal process

## 5. Security Measures

### 5.1 Technical Controls
- Encryption of data at rest and in transit
- Multi-factor authentication for administrators
- Regular security audits
- Penetration testing annually

### 5.2 Operational Controls
- Role-based access review quarterly
- Data access logging and monitoring
- Incident response plan
- Staff training on data privacy

## 6. Legal Compliance

### 6.1 Applicable Regulations
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for EU volunteers
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
- Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
- State-specific background check laws

### 6.2 Cross-border Data
- Data stored in US data centers
- Clear disclosure for international volunteers
- Alternative processes for GDPR compliance
