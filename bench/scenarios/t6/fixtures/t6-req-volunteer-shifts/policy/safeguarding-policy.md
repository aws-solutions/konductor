# Community Food Hub Safeguarding Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy outlines our commitment to protecting vulnerable individuals, including children, young people, and adults at risk, who access our services or participate in our programs.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all staff, volunteers, board members, and contractors working with or on behalf of Community Food Hub.

## 3. Key Principles

### 3.1 Child Protection
- Minimum age for volunteering: **16 years**
- Volunteers aged 16-17 require parental/guardian consent
- Volunteers under 16 may participate only in supervised family volunteering programs

### 3.2 Vulnerable Adults
- Special considerations for volunteers working with vulnerable adult populations
- Additional training required for kitchen and distribution roles at shelter sites

### 3.3 Background Checks
**Roles requiring background checks:**
1. Drivers (DMV check required)
2. Kitchen staff (food handler certification + background check)
3. Volunteers working directly with vulnerable populations at Northgate Shelter
4. Any role involving handling of cash or sensitive donor information

### 3.4 Supervision
- New volunteers must complete 3 shifts under direct supervision
- Ratio of experienced to new volunteers: minimum 1:2
- Site coordinators must conduct regular check-ins

## 4. Reporting Procedures
All safeguarding concerns must be reported to the Safeguarding Lead (Dr. Alisha Chen) within 24 hours.

## 5. Training Requirements
- All volunteers: Basic safeguarding awareness (online module)
- Site coordinators: Advanced safeguarding training (annual)
- Drivers: Defensive driving course (biannual)

## 6. Review
This policy is reviewed annually by the Board Safeguarding Committee.
