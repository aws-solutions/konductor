# Community Food Hub Volunteer Management

This folder contains documents and data related to our volunteer shift sign-up system project. We're moving from a shared Google Sheets spreadsheet to a proper digital sign-up system.

## Contents

- `data/` - Exported data from the current spreadsheet
- `policy/` - Organizational policies and guidelines
- `meetings/` - Meeting notes and planning documents
- `drafts/` - Previous drafts and planning documents
- `vendor/` - Vendor research and shortlists
- `communications/` - Newsletters and volunteer communications

## Project Goal

Replace the shared spreadsheet (volunteer-shifts-2024.csv) with a secure, web-based system that volunteers can access to sign up for shifts at our 6 distribution sites.
