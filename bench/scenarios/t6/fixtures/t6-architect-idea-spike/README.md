# Ledgerleaf: PDF / edge folder

Working folder for the "invoice PDFs at the edge" idea. Started by Marguerite after the 18 Sep product sync; Tomas and Priya added the engineering bits.

What is where:

- `pitch/`: the pitch as written, plus an older longer draft of it
- `architecture/`: how invoices turn into PDFs today (pipeline notes, the renderer ADR, the overview)
- `data/`: exports (render service latency, helpdesk tickets, tenant list). See `data/export-notes.txt` for how they were pulled
- `finance/`: monthly cost sheet and Ines's notes on it
- `vendor/`: what we collected about the edge vendor (limits, a sales email)
- `planning/`: team calendar, quarter freeze policy
- `incidents/`: postmortems
- `meetings/`: sync notes and an interview
- `support/`: current support targets, and the 2023 version kept for reference
- `sales/`: one prospect call
- `notes/`: engineering backlog scraps

Owners: product Marguerite Oyelaran, engineering Tomas Ribeiro, support Dana Kowalczyk, finance Ines Varga.

Some files are rough. Dates are 2025 unless said otherwise.
