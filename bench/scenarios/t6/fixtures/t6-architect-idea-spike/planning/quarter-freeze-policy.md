# Quarter-end freeze (excerpt from the engineering handbook)

Applies to: all engineers, every quarter.

1. The freeze starts on the first working day after the last full working week of the quarter. This quarter: Monday 29 September, running through Friday 10 October.
2. During the freeze engineers work only on the audit and release-hardening list owned by Tomas. New feature work, spikes and experiments are not scheduled in that period.
3. Work in flight must be merged, or parked with a written note in the repo, by end of day on the last working day before the freeze.
4. Incident fixes are exempt.
5. Leave booked before the freeze announcement is honoured; nobody is asked to cancel it.
6. Capacity numbers for planning are counted in whole engineer-days. A half day for a recurring meeting is not deducted unless the meeting is longer than an hour.
