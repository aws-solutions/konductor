How the exports in this folder were pulled (Priya, 16 Sep)

pdf-latency.csv
  - source: render service job log, 25 Aug 06:00 UTC to ~14 Sep
  - one row per job. `ms` is wall time from worker pickup to PDF written (queue wait not included)
  - `pages` comes from the finished PDF. A few rows have it blank (job log line was truncated)
  - `retry` means the job was re-queued once and this is the second attempt
  - `cold_start` = 1 when the worker had just started
  - I think a few duplicate lines slipped in when the log shipper restarted. Did not clean.
  - it is a sample of the log, not every job (log shipper drops some under load)

support-tickets.csv
  - helpdesk export, tickets created 2 Jun to 15 Sep
  - `invoice_pages` filled in by support when the ticket is about a specific invoice; blank otherwise
  - dates are mostly ISO, some agents typed dd/mm/yyyy, left as is
  - category is a free-pick from the helpdesk dropdown

tenant-plans.csv
  - from the billing DB, current plan and rough monthly invoice volume
