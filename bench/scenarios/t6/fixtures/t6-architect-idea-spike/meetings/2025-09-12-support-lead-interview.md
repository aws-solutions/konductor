# Chat with Dana Kowalczyk, support lead (Fri 12 Sep, ~25 min, my notes)

Why I asked: M. keeps hearing "PDFs are slow" and I wanted to know from whom.

- "Nobody writes to us about a normal invoice. People click, it's there."
- The complaints are statements. Monthly ones for the freight and wholesale customers. "Forty pages, sixty pages, the odd one that's over a hundred." They click download and watch a spinner. Some click again, which queues a second render.
- A few get the email attachment late instead.
- She keeps a sheet of who called; says it's the same handful of accounts. Business plan customers, who are the ones with the written support commitment.
- Target: we have a number written down for long invoices. "It's in the support targets doc, updated in March after the last review." She would be happy if we simply got inside it. Doesn't think "instant" matters for the long ones. "Nobody expects a 60-page statement to be instant. They expect it not to hang."
- Do they want it faster for small invoices? "Not that I've ever heard."
- Her team escalates to engineering when a statement takes over a minute. Happened a few times in summer.
- Worried about one thing: if a change makes some statements fail outright, that is worse than slow. They'd rather wait.

(didn't ask about volumes; ticket export coming)
