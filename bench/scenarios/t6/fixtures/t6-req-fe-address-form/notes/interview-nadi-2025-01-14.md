# Interview notes: Nadi Pell, customer support

Nadi has handled address tickets since the shop opened. Customers paste apartment details into the second line and then lose them after a failed lookup. People on phones often see the keyboard cover the postal-code hint. One customer used a parcel locker, but support could not tell whether that was a valid delivery address or a carrier exception.

Nadi's suggested copy: say what is wrong next to the field, keep the entered text, and do not make customers start over. She does not know which countries fulfilment can currently ship to.
