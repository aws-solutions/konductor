# Product accessibility handbook excerpt

Designers and engineers should use real labels, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and a logical keyboard order. Validation errors must be associated with their fields and announced without relying on color alone. The handbook is guidance for all product work; it does not name a conformance target for this checkout snapshot.

When a form is redesigned, record any product-specific accessibility commitments in the requirements rather than assuming that the handbook's general guidance is a testable contract.
