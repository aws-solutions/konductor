# Northmere Field Inspection App

This folder is the working handover for replacing paper inspection forms used by Northmere Water Utility crews. It includes the current form, operating notes, policy excerpts, exports from the asset and inspection systems, and a few older drafts.

The material was collected by the product group during the winter planning cycle. It is not a clean specification: some notes are personal recollections, exports have blank or inconsistent values, and the older draft contains decisions that were never approved. Asset names in the exports are abbreviated. Dates in operational exports are generally the date on the paper form, not necessarily the upload date.

The first release is expected to support field capture and the records team's handoff. The planning group still needs a usable requirements document that distinguishes current rules from unresolved choices.
