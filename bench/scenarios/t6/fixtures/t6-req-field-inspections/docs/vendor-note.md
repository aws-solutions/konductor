# Vendor discovery note

2025-02-05, short call with Blue Orchard Systems. This is a discovery note, not a quote or commitment.

The vendor demonstrated a local form cache, background upload, attachment retries, and a device camera handoff. The demonstration used a single tablet and a stable Wi-Fi network. It did not cover two edits to one record, a device changing hands, or a stop-work visit.

The vendor said Android storage limits and MDM policies should be confirmed during technical discovery. They also said a camera file can remain at its captured size, but the utility would need to decide its storage rule.
