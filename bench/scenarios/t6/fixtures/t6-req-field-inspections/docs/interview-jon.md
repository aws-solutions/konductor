# Interview notes: Jon Bell, inspector

2025-01-13, ride-along debrief. Jon has worked the north route for six seasons.

Jon said the paper sheet is manageable until a defect needs evidence. He normally takes a few pictures with the crew camera, then writes a short reference on the form. The pictures sometimes arrive in a separate email, and Records has to guess which asset they belong to.

He wants the asset tag and site name visible while he is entering a finding. He also wants a way to mark a check as not applicable without putting a misleading number in the reading box.

He said the app should not make people stand in a bad location just to finish a photo. A visit can be left incomplete and explained later. Jon was unsure whether photographs should be resized for storage or retained as taken.
