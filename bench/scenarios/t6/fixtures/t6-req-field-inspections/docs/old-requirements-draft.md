# Field capture draft, not approved

Drafted 2024-11-22 by the delivery office. Do not treat this as an approved decision.

1. A worker can select an asset from a route list and complete the same broad sections as the paper form.
2. The app should work in offline mode and upload later.
3. A supervisor can review submitted records.
4. Photos should be attached to findings.
5. The app should use the device clock and show the current site.

Questions in the margin include whether a supervisor is the same person as the field lead, whether a record can be edited after upload, and how duplicate submissions are handled. The draft says “near real time” in one place and “end of day” in another. Neither phrase was signed off.
