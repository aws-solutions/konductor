# Paper inspection form, revision 3

Printed form used by maintenance and compliance crews. A single form is used for one asset visit.

Header fields:
- inspection reference (written by the depot clerk)
- asset tag and asset type
- site name and access point
- inspector name and crew
- visit date and arrival/departure time

Condition section:
- condition code: OK, MONITOR, REPAIR, or OUT OF SERVICE
- observed reading, where the asset type has a reading field
- free-text observations
- corrective action or work-order reference

Evidence section:
- mark whether a photo was taken
- photo reference(s), if any
- note why evidence could not be captured

The paper copy has an inspector signature and a field-lead review box. The lead initials the box after looking over the completed form. Forms with an OUT OF SERVICE code are clipped to the red tray at the depot. The bottom of the form has a space for a records clerk to write the filing location.

The form does not explain what to do when a visit is interrupted or when two copies exist for one asset. It also does not define whether a blank reading means zero, not applicable, or missed.
