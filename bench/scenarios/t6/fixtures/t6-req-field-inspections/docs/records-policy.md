# Records policy excerpt: operational inspection records

Policy RP-14, approved 2024-06-11.

Completed inspection records and their audit history are retained for 7 years from the record's completion date. A correction creates a new audit event; the original submitted value must remain recoverable. Records staff may place a legal hold that suspends normal disposal.

The system of record stores a record identifier, asset identifier, event times, submitting worker, review events, and attachments. An export must preserve the relationship between an inspection and its evidence. Deletion from a tablet is not deletion from the system of record.

The policy permits a record to be entered without a reading when the field is not applicable, but the reason must be distinguishable from a missed entry. Time values in the archive are stored with timezone information.
