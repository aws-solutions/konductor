# Safety discussion notes

2025-01-16, toolbox talk with Rhea Sol and two crew representatives.

The existing induction says that an inspector may stop work for a dangerous condition, move to a safe location, and contact the field lead. The paper form has no dedicated stop-work mark, so crews write “visit stopped” across the condition section.

Examples discussed in the talk included strong chemical fumes, live wiring, poor footing near an open chamber, flooding, and aggressive animals. The exact hazard vocabulary belongs to Safety and may change with the annual induction.

A stopped visit must remain visible to the lead even if the normal condition checks are blank. Do not make a person return to a hazard to complete a photo or reading. The group did not settle whether the app should require a reason before it can be saved.
