# Interview notes: Mara Venn, field lead

2025-01-09, depot meeting room. Notes by Isha Pell.

Mara runs the rotating inspection crews and is the person people call when the route plan falls apart. She wants the app to feel quicker than the clipboard, not like a second office system.

- Crews work around pump stations, intakes, and reservoir sites. Some roads have no dependable service. A form must not disappear because the crew crossed a ridge.
- Tablets are kept at the depot and are passed between crews. The morning crew and the late crew do not always have the same people.
- A lead needs to see problems before the weekly planning meeting. Mara does not want an app that quietly changes an earlier observation.
- “If the place is unsafe, the visit is over. The crew calls me. We can fill in the administrative parts later.”
- The current form's signature box is often completed back at the depot. There is no agreed digital equivalent yet.

Open in the margin: decide how a form is identified before it reaches Records. Also decide who can correct a typo after upload.
