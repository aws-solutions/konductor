# Travel and Subsistence Policy

**Effective:** 1 January 2023
**Applies to:** All staff, volunteers, trustees

## 1. General Principles

Travel should be:
- Necessary for charity business
- Booked in advance where possible
- Using the most cost-effective option
- Approved before travel (except emergencies)

## 2. Travel Booking

### 2.1 Air Travel
- Economy class for all flights
- Advance booking (minimum 14 days where possible)
- No business class without ED approval

### 2.2 Rail Travel
- Standard class for journeys under 2 hours
- First class only if standard full and urgent
- Advance tickets preferred

### 2.3 Car Travel
- Personal car mileage may be claimed
- Charity-owned vehicles booked through office
- Taxis only when no reasonable alternative

## 3. Accommodation

### 3.1 Hotel Standards
- Budget hotels preferred
- Maximum £120/night in London
- Maximum £90/night elsewhere
- Breakfast included where possible

### 3.2 Approval
- Pre-approval required for all accommodation
- Conference hotels may exceed limits with justification

## 4. Subsistence

### 4.1 Meal Allowances
- Breakfast: £8 (if not provided)
- Lunch: £12
- Dinner: £25
- Maximum £45/day total

### 4.2 Receipts Required
- Individual meals over £15
- Total daily subsistence over £30
- All alcohol must be separately justified

## 5. International Travel

Special rules apply for international travel:
- Medical insurance required
- Security briefing for high-risk areas
- ED approval for all international travel
- Per diem rates vary by country (see separate schedule)

## 6. Mileage Rates

Current rates (reviewed annually):
- Car: 45p per mile (first 10,000 miles), 25p thereafter
- Motorcycle: 24p per mile
- Bicycle: 20p per mile

**Note:** These rates include all running costs (fuel, insurance, maintenance, depreciation). No separate claims for parking, tolls, or congestion charge.

## 7. Advances

Travel advances available:
- Up to 80% of estimated costs
- Require ED approval
- Must be reconciled within 14 days of return

## 8. Compliance

- Claims must be submitted within 30 days
- Original receipts required for all claims
- Policy exceptions require written approval
