# BrightHope Charity - Finance Handbook (Excerpt)

**Version:** 3.2
**Effective:** 1 January 2023
**Last reviewed:** December 2023

## Chapter 7: Expense Claims

### 7.1 General Principles

All expenses must be:
- **Necessary** for charity business
- **Reasonable** in amount
- **Properly authorised** before being incurred (where practical)
- **Supported** by original receipts or equivalent evidence

### 7.2 Authorisation Limits

Claims must be approved by:
- **Line manager** for all claims
- **Head of Finance** for claims over £500
- **Executive Director** for claims over £2,000

*Note: The Board must approve any single claim exceeding £5,000.*

### 7.3 Submission Deadlines

Claims should be submitted within **30 days** of the expense being incurred. Claims older than 90 days will not be reimbursed except in exceptional circumstances approved by the Head of Finance.

### 7.4 Receipt Requirements

Original receipts must be provided for:
- All accommodation expenses
- All travel expenses over £25
- All meal expenses over £15 per person
- All equipment purchases

For expenses under these thresholds, a brief description is sufficient.

### 7.5 Travel Expenses

- Economy class travel should be used for all journeys
- Staff may claim for reasonable subsistence while travelling on charity business
- Personal car mileage may be claimed at the approved rate (see separate notice)

### 7.6 Grant-Funded Expenses

Expenses charged to restricted grants must:
- Be directly related to the grant objectives
- Comply with any specific grant conditions
- Use the correct grant coding (see Grant Administration Manual)

### 7.7 Payment Schedule

Approved claims are paid:
- Via bank transfer on the **15th** of each month for claims approved by the 5th
- Via bank transfer on the **last working day** for claims approved between the 6th and 20th

### 7.8 Record Retention

All expense claim documentation must be retained for **7 years** for audit purposes.
