## Approval Hierarchy

### Expense Claims
1. **Line Manager** (any amount)
2. **Head of Finance** (> £500)
3. **Executive Director** (> £2,000)
4. **Board** (> £5,000 - exceptional)

### Delegation Rules
- When line manager absent: Deputy or department head
- When Head of Finance absent: Executive Director
- When Executive Director absent: Board Chair (for >£2,000 claims)
