# Active Grants and Restrictions

**Last updated:** 1 March 2024
**Maintained by:** Sarah Chen, Head of Finance

## Grant Codes and Purposes

### GEN - General Funds
- **Purpose:** Unrestricted operations
- **Donor:** Multiple
- **Period:** Ongoing
- **Restrictions:** None
- **Budget:** £1,200,000

### YEP - Youth Empowerment Programme  
- **Purpose:** Youth training and employment projects
- **Donor:** Community Foundation
- **Period:** 1 Jan 2023 - 31 Dec 2025
- **Restrictions:** 
  - No international travel
  - Maximum £75/day per person for subsistence (2023 agreement; renewal under negotiation)
  - Equipment purchases require pre-approval
  - No alcohol expenses
- **Budget:** £850,000

### CEP - Community Engagement Programme
- **Purpose:** Community development activities
- **Donor:** National Lottery
- **Period:** 1 Apr 2022 - 31 Mar 2026
- **Restrictions:**
  - UK travel only
  - No hotel accommodation over £120/night (2022 agreement; renewal under negotiation)
  - Training costs limited to 15% of project budget
  - No venue hire over £500 without prior approval
- **Budget:** £650,000

### RES - Research Project
- **Purpose:** Impact assessment study
- **Donor:** Academic Trust
- **Period:** 1 Sep 2023 - 31 Aug 2024
- **Restrictions:**
  - Research staff only
  - Data collection expenses only
  - No conference attendance
  - Maximum £1,000 for equipment
- **Budget:** £95,000

## Coding Rules

1. Always use the most specific grant code applicable
2. When multiple grants could apply, use GEN as default
3. Training expenses should use GEN unless specifically grant-funded
4. Office overheads (rent, utilities) use GEN
5. Staff salaries split proportionally across active projects

## Important Notes

- Some grants have daily rate caps that aren't in our current system
- The YEP grant has a special rule about advance approval for equipment over £300
- CEP doesn't allow any expenses related to fundraising
- All restricted grants require quarterly reporting
