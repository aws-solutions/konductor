# Internal Audit Report - Expense Claims Process

**Audit Date:** 15 November 2023
**Auditor:** Green & Associates Chartered Accountants
**Period Covered:** 1 January 2023 - 31 October 2023
**Report to:** BrightHope Charity Board Finance Committee

## Executive Summary

We conducted a review of the expense claims process and identified several control weaknesses that require attention. While no fraud was detected, the manual nature of the process creates significant risk.

## Key Findings

### 1. Lack of Segregation of Duties
- The same finance staff member can both approve and process payments for claims under £500
- No secondary review for claims between £500 and £2,000

### 2. Grant Compliance Issues
- 8 claims (total £1,240) were incorrectly charged to restricted grants
- No systematic check that expenses comply with grant terms
- Staff receive insufficient training on grant restrictions

### 3. Approval Delegation Gaps
- No documented procedure for approving claims when primary approver is absent
- During July 2023, 12 claims were delayed 7+ days due to approver holiday

### 4. Receipt Documentation
- 15% of claims reviewed lacked proper receipts
- No policy on accepting digital/photographed receipts
- Receipt retention inconsistent

### 5. Self-Approval Risks
- Managers approving their own expenses without oversight (3 instances found)
- No upper limit for self-approval

### 6. Mileage Claims
- No verification of business purpose for mileage
- Rate used (45p/mile) not formally approved by Board
- No distinction between different vehicle types

### 7. Timing Controls
- No enforcement of 30-day submission deadline
- Claims processed outside normal payment runs create manual work

## Recommendations

1. **Immediate (Q1 2024):** Establish clear delegation procedures for absent approvers
2. **Short-term (Q2 2024):** Implement system to validate grant coding
3. **Medium-term (2024):** Automate approval workflows with proper segregation
4. **Ongoing:** Provide staff training on grant restrictions

## Management Response

*We acknowledge the findings and have allocated budget for a new expense claims system in 2024. The system will address most recommendations through automated controls.*

**Sarah Chen, Head of Finance**
