# Interview Notes: Staff Feedback on Expense Claims

**Dates:** 25-28 March 2024
**Interviewer:** Mark Taylor
**Method:** One-on-one conversations, 8 staff members across departments

## Common Themes

### Frustrations
- "No one knows when I'll get paid" - Field Officer
- "I have to chase my manager, then finance, it's embarrassing" - Programme Assistant
- "The form is confusing - which grant code should I use?" - Fundraising Manager
- "Receipts get lost in my bag" - Regional Coordinator

### Suggestions
- Push notifications when claim status changes
- Simple mobile app to take receipt photos immediately
- Clear guidance on grant coding
- Faster approval for small amounts
- Visibility of who has the claim and when it will move

## Specific Issues Raised

### Grant Coding
- Staff confused about which grants allow which expenses
- No warning when trying to charge to a restricted grant
- Some grants have daily rate limits that aren't checked

### Approvals
- When James is on leave, no one knows who can approve
- Large claims (>£2,000) need ED approval, but she's often travelling
- Self-approval for tiny amounts? (under £10)

### Receipts
- Field staff lose paper receipts
- Photos should be acceptable for all claims
- Need guidance on what constitutes a valid receipt

### Mileage
- Current rate seems low compared to HMRC
- No calculator - staff do math manually
- Should we track business vs personal miles?

### Timing
- Payment dates unclear
- Claims submitted just after cutoff miss payment run
- No reminder for 30-day submission deadline

## Quotes

"I submitted a claim in January, still waiting. It was only £45." - Admin Assistant

"The grant restrictions spreadsheet is 15 pages. I just guess." - Project Officer

"When Sarah's away, everything stops. We need backup approvers." - Operations Manager

"I claimed mileage for 200 miles last month. Took me 20 minutes to calculate." - Field Coordinator

## Recommendations from Staff
1. Clear, simple interface
2. Mobile-first design
3. Automatic calculations
4. Transparent process
5. Quick approval for small claims
6. Better guidance on policies
