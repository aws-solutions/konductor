# Project Brief: Expense Claims System

**Date:** 15 March 2024
**Author:** Sarah Chen, Head of Finance
**Status:** Draft for review

## Objective

Replace the current manual email-based expense claim process with a digital system that:
1. Reduces processing time from current average of 14 days to under 5 days
2. Improves accuracy of coding and approvals
3. Provides real-time visibility into claim status
4. Ensures compliance with charity regulations and grant restrictions
5. Integrates with our existing accounting software (Xero)

## Scope

- All staff expense claims (travel, subsistence, equipment, training)
- All approval workflows (line manager, finance, executive director)
- Receipt capture and validation
- Grant coding and restriction enforcement
- Reporting and analytics

## Out of Scope

- Payroll integration (separate project)
- Procurement system (future phase)
- Donor management (already handled by DonorPerfect)

## Timeline

- Requirements gathering: April 2024
- Vendor selection: May-June 2024
- Implementation: July-September 2024
- Go-live: October 2024

## Success Metrics

- 80% reduction in manual data entry
- 95% of claims processed within 5 working days
- Zero coding errors for restricted grants
- Staff satisfaction score > 4/5
