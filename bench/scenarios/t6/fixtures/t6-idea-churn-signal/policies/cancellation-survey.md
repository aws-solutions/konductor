# Cancellation survey excerpt

The survey is shown after a member confirms the cancellation action. It is not shown when a subscription ends because an unpaid invoice reaches the end of its grace period. Responses are optional and are stored with the cancellation-flow visit.

The survey is intended to collect language and reasons for voluntary exits. It is not a census of all churn routes. Product may use the responses to improve the cancellation experience, but the responses must not be used alone to estimate the overall cause of lost subscriptions.
