# Experiment notes from the growth handbook

A retention change should keep a holdout or a clear comparison. Report retained subscribers and collected revenue separately. Do not count a delayed invoice as a retained paid subscriber.

Any price change needs a margin check. A discount shown only to people who already chose to cancel can create selection effects, so compare later payment and cancellation behavior with the existing path.

When a tracking outage affects a route, mark that period as incomplete instead of treating the raw event count as a drop in demand.
