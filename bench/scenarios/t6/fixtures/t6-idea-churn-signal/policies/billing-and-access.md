# Billing and access policy excerpt

**Owner:** Rina Shah, finance
**Effective:** 2025-02-01

1. When a card charge fails, the billing service records the failure and emails the member a card-update link.
2. The service does not automatically retry the processor charge. A member may update a card or contact support.
3. Access remains available until the invoice grace period ends. The current grace period is 3 days.
4. At grace expiry, the invoice is closed unpaid and the subscription is ended unless a payment has posted.
5. A payment made by support or through the update link is labelled `manual_payment` or `card_updated`; it must not be counted as an automated retry.

The policy does not prescribe a cancellation discount. Finance approval is required for any offer that changes the member's price.
