# Member interview synthesis

**Prepared by:** Ana Torres, research
**Date:** 2025-06-20
**Participants:** 8 members contacted through recent support conversations and 4 members who completed a cancellation flow.

People who wanted to leave mentioned price, unused boxes, and a change in household size. Two people said they would have paused for a month if the choice had been visible. Three people described updating a card after a payment notice. One person thought the first notice meant a second charge was already scheduled.

This is directional. The recruitment list came from support and a cancellation-flow export, so it does not represent members who silently lost access after a failed payment. The interviews also happened before the June ledger was reconciled.

Quotes and participant notes are in the three small files beside this one. They are useful for language, not for estimating the share of churn causes.
