## Working definitions

A subscriber is counted as lost when the paid relationship ends or access is closed after an unpaid invoice. A billing failure can remain open during the three-day grace period. The dashboard's churn snapshot uses a seven-day look-forward so late payment recovery is not counted as churn too early.

The June snapshot covers 2025-06-01 through 2025-06-30. The product dashboard was refreshed on 2025-07-03. The billing export is event-level; some subscribers have more than one failure event.

The team has not agreed whether a payment-recovery experience and a voluntary cancellation save offer should share a surface. The request uses “save offer” as shorthand, not as a decision.
