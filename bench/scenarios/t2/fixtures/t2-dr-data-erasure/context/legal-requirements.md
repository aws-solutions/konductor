# Legal and policy requirements

The privacy operations brief for the launch uses a 30-calendar-day response period for a verified consumer deletion request. The clock starts when the request is verified, not when a worker picks it up. A regional holiday does not pause the period. The service may put a request on a documented legal hold, but the hold must be visible to privacy operations and released through the case system.

The response to the consumer must state whether deletion is complete and identify any data that is retained under an exception. The organization must be able to show the request, verification result, actions taken, exceptions, and completion time without retaining more personal data than needed for that evidence. The privacy operations team can access an internal request reference and account identifier; it does not need the original email address or display name in the audit record.

Financial ledger entries, abuse-prevention signals, and records covered by an active legal hold have documented exceptions. An exception is a retention decision, not a reason to leave unrelated profile data in place. Requests must be authenticated again when submitted from a session older than 12 hours.
