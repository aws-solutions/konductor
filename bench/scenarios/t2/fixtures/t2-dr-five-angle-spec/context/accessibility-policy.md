# Product accessibility policy

Form validation errors must be available as text, associated with the field that needs correction, and announced to users of assistive technology when validation occurs. A change in colour, border, or icon may reinforce an error but cannot be the only signal.

The invite form has a visible label for each control. The email field accepts one address at a time, and the submit control remains in the form after a validation response. The policy applies to keyboard and screen-reader use on supported web clients.
