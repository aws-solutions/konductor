   - `expert-key.json`: what judges use beside your key, never shown to the agent. Shape: `{ "items": [ { "id": "e1", "text": "...", "source": "..." } ], "weakAnswers": ["...", "...", "..."] }`.
     - 5 to 8 `items`: the risks, trade-offs, constraints and failure modes a strong review of THIS document raises that are NOT already planted items in `key.json` (do not repeat them). Each `text` is one sentence a judge can check against a review ("Asks how the service sheds load when the vendor slows down and what is dropped first"). Each `source` names the public source the idea comes from, with its licence, from this list only: `https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/ (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0)`, `https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org (CC BY-SA 4.0)`, `https://12factor.net (MIT)`, `https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9700 (IETF Trust)`, `https://github.com/kubernetes/enhancements (Apache-2.0)`, `https://github.com/adr/madr (MIT OR CC0-1.0)`. Take the idea only; quote nothing.
     - 3 to 5 `weakAnswers`: what a shallow review of this document would say (generic praise, "consider scalability", restating the design).
     - Judges read this file, the script does not; keep it valid JSON.
