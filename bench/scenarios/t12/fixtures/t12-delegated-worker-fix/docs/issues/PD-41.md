# PD-41: pound weights are wrong, and capital units are refused

Reported by: Ines (counter, Leeds)

Parcels weighed in pounds come out light. The scale says 3 lb, the quote says 1.35 kg; a pound is 0.45359237 kg, so
3 lb is 1.361 kg (we round to 3 decimals, as everywhere). On heavy parcels this puts them in the wrong price band:
10 lb should be 4.536 kg and priced as such.

Also, the new scales print the unit in capitals (`10 LB`, `2 KG`), and the counter refuses those with "bad weight".
Units should be accepted in any case: `kg`, `KG`, `Kg`, `lb`, `LB`, `g`, `G` all work.

Still refused, as today: anything that is not a number followed by one of the units, such as `ten lb` or `5 stone`.
