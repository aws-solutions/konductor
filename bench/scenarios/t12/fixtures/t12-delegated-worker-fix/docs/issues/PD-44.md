# PD-44: delivery dates land on weekends and holidays

Reported by: Marek (customer service)

A parcel sent standard on Friday 6 March 2026 got a delivery date of Monday 9 March. Standard is 3 business days, so
it should be Wednesday 11 March. Saturdays and Sundays are not business days, and neither are the dates in
`config/holidays.json`.

Express over Easter is the worst: sent on Thursday 2 April 2026, it was promised for Friday 3 April, which is Good
Friday. The first business day after it is Tuesday 7 April (Easter Monday is a holiday too).

Counting starts the day after the ship date, as it does today. A holiday that falls on a weekend does not push the
date out any further than the weekend already does.
