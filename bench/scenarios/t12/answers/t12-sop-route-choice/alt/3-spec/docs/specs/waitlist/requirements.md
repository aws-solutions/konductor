## Rules agreed in the channel

| Id | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| R1 | A guest may join the waitlist for a room and date only when that room is fully booked on that date. |
| R2 | Each room and date takes five guests at most. |
| R3 | A cancellation for the room and date sends an offer to the guest at the head of the list. |
| R4 | The offer lapses two hours after it is sent; the guest after them is offered next. |
| R5 | A guest can withdraw from the list. |
