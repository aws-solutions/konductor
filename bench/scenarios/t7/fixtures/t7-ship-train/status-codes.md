# Ticket export field notes

The export uses status text as written by the delivery board. Empty cells are preserved. `Ready` means acceptance criteria are available; `Blocked` means the ticket remains in the active queue but cannot currently move. `Duplicate` is retained for audit history and is not active work.

The `initiative` and `workstream` columns are maintained separately. The initiative identifies the roadmap grouping; the workstream identifies the team doing the work.
