# Platform roadmap

As of 2027-02-01, the new storage engine is tracked as the **Storage Engine** initiative in the ticket export. Its intended customer capability is the segment store, replay, health reporting, compaction, and the service read path. Archive Migration is a separate initiative that has its own retention-export objective.

The roadmap does not change the ticket estimates or status values. A release train is considered to contain work that is merged to `main` by its branch date.

The engine is intended to replace the compatibility path after release review.
