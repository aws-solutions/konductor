# Backlog export notes

The export includes open work and historical rows. Count `Open`, `Ready`, `In Progress`, and `Blocked` as open for planning. Do not plan rows marked `Done`, `Closed`, `Cancelled`, or `Canceled`.

`estimate_points` is blank when the ticket has no usable estimate. A blank is not zero. `depends_on` is a semicolon-separated list of ticket IDs; an empty cell means no recorded dependency. Names in the roster are authoritative when matching people.
