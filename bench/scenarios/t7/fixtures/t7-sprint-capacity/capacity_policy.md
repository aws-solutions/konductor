# Capacity notes

A sprint includes every Monday through Friday from its inclusive start through inclusive end. A listed holiday removes that working day for everybody. For each remaining day, a person's capacity is `base_points_per_workday * focus_factor`; a person on call gets half of that day's capacity. Sprint capacity is the sum of all persons' daily capacity.

A ticket is assigned to the only roster person with the matching skill when there is one.
